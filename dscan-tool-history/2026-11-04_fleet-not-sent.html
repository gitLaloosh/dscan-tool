Why

  • The owner asked whether one failed request fails the rest. It does, on purpose: after one zKillboard lookup fails, the pause holds every later one back without sending it (so the address isn't blocked, LESSON #93). But every row said "zKillboard isn't answering (ZKB-01)" alike. The owner: "build it. it's giving the impression that that tool just doesn't work."

What changed

  • zkbFetchNow's error for a lookup held back by the pause carries notSent; the one that reached zKillboard and failed doesn't.
  • On the Fleet tab, the row whose lookup failed stays red ("zKillboard isn't answering (ZKB-01)"). The rows held back are dim: "Not sent: zKillboard needs a rest (ZKB-01)", and the hover says why (the page holds the rest back until zKillboard answers, so your address isn't blocked; Load or Load all asks again when the wait ends).
  • With no pilot loaded, the summary says it plainly: "zKillboard isn't answering: 1 lookup failed and the other 10 weren't sent, so your address isn't blocked. Load all asks again when the wait ends."
  • Nothing about when requests are sent changed.
