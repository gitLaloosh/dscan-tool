Bug fixed: cap booster reload

pyfa's capacitor sim always applies an injector's reload, even when reload factoring is off. In eos/capSim.py, the clip size is set to infinite when reloads are disabled, except for injectors.

  • Clip size = floor(module capacity / charge volume). The Capital Capacitor Booster II holds 640 m³, and a Cap Booster 3200 is 128 m³, so 5 charges fit. Reload time (attr 1795) is 10 s.
  • Average injection = n × GJ / (n × cycle + reload) = 16,000 / 70 = 228.6 GJ/s. pyfa shows "16k / 60s (+10s)" and +229.

The new capBoosterAvgGainPerSec() applies this to all three paths: a flag-paired charge, a cargo-inferred charge, and the no-charge range. In the range path, each candidate charge gets its own clip size. If the volume or reload attribute is missing, it falls back to the old no-reload rate.

Biggest effect is on small and medium boosters, which often hold just one large charge. The Curse loss's Medium F-RX Compact holds a single Navy Cap Booster 800, so it injects 800 / 22.75 s = 35.2 GJ/s, not 62.7. That fit is cap-unstable again even with its booster running.

Shield rep: the difference is boosters (drugs), not a bug

pyfa shows 85,426.6 EHP/s of shield repair. Under the uniform damage profile that is about 17,030 HP/s raw. The tool shows 12,018 HP/s raw.

The ratio is 1.417, which is exactly Erik's two boosters: Strong Blue Pill (+30% shield boost amount) × Agency 'Hardshell' TB9 Dose IV (+9%) = 1.30 × 1.09. Both use pyfa effect shieldBoostAmplifierPassiveBooster.

This tool models neither implants nor boosters. That's already logged as the Fit Analyzer implants/boosters backlog item, now extended to cover boosters too.
