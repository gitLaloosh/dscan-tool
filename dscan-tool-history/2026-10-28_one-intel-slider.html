What changed

  • The Intel card has no slider now; its gear stays where it was. The Intel slider at the top, beside Standings, is the only on/off switch, on every tab.
  • The card's first-time text says "The Intel slider at the top, beside Standings, starts and stops it from any tab"; stopped, it says "Stopped. The Intel slider at the top starts it again." The info panel says the same.
  • The lists in the Intel settings (alert range, how long reports stay) were unreadable when opened on Windows: the owner's screenshot of v.96 showed near-white numbers on a white list. The selects had a transparent background, and Windows draws an open list with each option's own colours, so the options got the select's light text on the system's white. They now have the page's dark background (and color-scheme: dark), and the options a dark background with light text.
  • The "N near" pill: the owner: "probably need to move the notification pill. i accidently turned intel off thinking the pill was clickable." The pill sat inside the slider's label, and a press anywhere in a label presses its control, so the pill switched Intel off. On a phone it stays on one line and its button is 44 px tall.
  • Nothing else about Intel changed.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #84: one state, one control. When a mockup puts a switch somewhere global, check every page where it's already visible before adding a local copy there. A second place may show the state, but it shouldn't be a second switch. And anything put inside a switch's <label> becomes part of that switch: a badge that looks pressable there will flip it. Give a badge its own element and its own action.
