My Ships: several saved fits

FeatureTests 2026-10-25 · v2026.10.14.64 → v2026.10.14.65. Mockup: four screenshots of the real page (panel and Matchup menu, desktop and phone).

Backlog item "Several saved fits for My Ship" (the owner via the inbox, 2026-10-24: "add ability to store multiple fits for 'my ship'"). The owner's decisions, 2026-10-25:

  • switch in the panel and on the Matchup;
  • no "compare all my fits" view ("Not needed");
  • most recently used first;
  • picked from the list, "it should become the active ship everywhere";
  • "let's limit the list of fits they can save to 20 for now".

What you see

  • The panel (the chip) is now "My Ships":
    • the saved fits, most recently used first, each with its class and when it was last used;
    • "In use" on the one in use, a Use button on the others, and "+ Add a fit".
    Click a fit to see its six numbers. A fit that isn't in use is computed for the panel too, with "Use this ship". Then come Rename, Replace fit, Copy EFT and Delete.
  • The chip shows the ship in use and "+N" for the others.
  • The Matchup board: your ship's name is a menu of your saved fits, with a check on the one in use and "Manage My Ships". On a phone it's a bottom sheet, "Your ship for this matchup". Picking a fit there, or Use in the panel, makes it the ship in use everywhere:
    • the chip;
    • every board and its numbers;
    • the Damage Application cards;
    • the Fit Analyzer's "Viewing my ship" page.
    The side shown (threat or my ship) doesn't change.
  • Each fit keeps its own module, drone and mode states. "Use in assessments" is one switch for whichever fit is in use.
  • Adding and replacing:
    • pasting a fit that's already saved adds nothing; the saved one is used;
    • "Set as my ship" in the Fit Analyzer adds the fit and uses it. It reads "Use as my ship" when that fit is already saved;
    • replacing a fit that isn't in use keeps the ship in use;
    • deleting the ship in use passes to the next most recent.
  • Rename changes the name everywhere and in Copy EFT's header. The stored EFT text stays as pasted.
  • 20 fits at most. At 20, "+ Add a fit" and "Set as my ship" are disabled, and a paste is refused with the new error code MYSHIP-03 ("My Ships holds 20 fits. Delete one to add another.").

How it's stored

  • localStorage.dscanMyShips = {v: 2, inUse: id, use, fits: [...]}. Each fit is the v.33 record plus an id and the time it was last used.
  • A ship saved by v.33–v.64 (dscanMyShip) is read as the first entry. dscanMyShip is still written as a copy of the ship in use, so an older build of the page still finds it.
  • The numbers are still never stored. Each saved fit is computed from its EFT the first time it's needed in a page load, then cached by id, so switching back is instant.

Presentation and inputs only. No calculation changed, and no PYFA CORE section was touched.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #60: five page checks wait for the Matchup's numbers by testing that the board has no "…" in it. The first menu ended in "Manage My Ships…", and every one of them waited forever.

  • Before adding text to an element the checks read, grep the checks for what they look for.
  • To hide text that must keep its size and stay in a button's accessible name, use color:transparent, not visibility:hidden. The selector must be more specific than the rule that colours that text; a same-specificity rule earlier in the file loses.
  • Measure that the overlay sits on it.
