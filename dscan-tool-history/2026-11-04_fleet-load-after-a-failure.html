Fleet tab: Load and Load all after a failed lookup

Page 2026-11-04 · v2026.11.04.5

Why

  • The owner, testing v2026.11.04.4: "retry should probably be the same "Load" and "Load All" buttons. modify the message to indicate the nature of the issue and show the retry timer on the buttons themselves. doable?" His screenshot also showed "All loaded" while 11 rows had failed ("that's what drew my attention to it"). No mockup (the owner's choice: "skip").

What changed

  • A failed lookup leaves the pilot unloaded, with why on the row, instead of a separate error state: "zKillboard isn't answering (ZKB-01)", "zKillboard limit reached in this browser (ZKB-04)", or any other failure as its own message says. The full message is the hover.
  • Their Load is back in the Fit column. While zKillboard can't be asked (its pause, or the page's 100 in 10 minutes) it is disabled and counts down, "Load in 0:42"; at the end the tab is drawn again and it says "Load". A row whose fit needs no request (kept from the last hour, or the share link's) isn't held back.
  • Load all covers the failed pilots too, with its exact cost, and counts down the same way ("Load all in 0:42"). The Retry in the card's head is gone.
  • "All loaded" only when every pilot is loaded. With any failed, Load all stays. When none could be looked up, the summary says why and points at Load all.
  • The row's cost now uses Load all's rule: a pilot whose own kept losses already hold one in that hull needs no request for their group's list. Before, the row said "1 zKillboard request" while Load all rightly counted none (LESSON #107: one meaning worked out twice drifts).
  • No new requests, timers or stored data; the countdowns use the one 1 s tick (v2026.11.04.4).
