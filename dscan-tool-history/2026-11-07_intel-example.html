An example of Intel for new users

Page 2026-11-07 · v2026.11.07.7

Why

  • Build queue 017, Project doc roadmap/intel-example-plan.md, mockup Claude outputs/intel_example_mockup.html (rebuilt here with the Project's scripts). The owner: "new users should have an idea of what intel will look like once they've set up their profile, linked their intel channels and the feed starts picking up data"; "Still picture with callouts", "Below the setup steps"; the notes "looks good".
  • The build review (Project doc roadmap/intel-example-build-review.md) found that the switch turned Intel on with no channel ticked. The owner: "why would we allow intel to be turned out if no intel channels have been linked and chosen?", and "fix it in 017". He also chose where the example goes on a quiet start ("Below the empty lists") and that "gone for good" is remembered "only if the user currently has their chat logs linked".

What changed

  • The example: under the setup steps, a dashed frame tagged EXAMPLE: the page's own Map, Near You and the Feed's first 5 rows, drawn by the real builders from a small made-up set (IT_EXAMPLE: 13 pilots, 4 corporations, 3 alliances, 7 reports and 3 kills on real systems around 1DQ1-A), with five numbered markers and their notes. It can't be pressed or tabbed into (inert). Its ages were worked out once; nothing in it moves.
  • Nothing leaks: while it is built, Intel's own state, your Standings and the map's state are set aside and put back exactly; its reports count as history, so they never sound or notify. Nothing about it is asked of ESI or zKillboard: its made-up groups have negative ids, and their names, tickers and emblems (small inline shields) come from the page itself. Only ship icons load, as in the real Feed.
  • When: shown before setup, during setup, and on a quiet start (below the empty lists). Gone once the first real report or kill arrives, and remembered in this browser (dscanItExGone, new; an older page never reads it) while the chat log folder is linked; with no folder linked it shows again. "See an example" in Intel's info panel brings it back, with the shared close button.
  • Browsers without the folder picker: an amber note above the steps from the start ("Intel needs Chrome or Edge on a computer"). In Brave, which ships the picker turned off, the note says how to turn it on (brave://flags/#file-system-access-api, as text to copy); INTEL-01 says the same.
  • Bug fixed: the switch no longer turns Intel on with no intel channel ticked ("Choose your intel channels first (2 below)."), and unticking the last channel stops it.

Checks

  • New intel_example_check.py: shown and hidden at each step, the markers on their targets at 1440 and 390, no request or storage write or sound, Intel's and the map's state unchanged, inert, emblems and tickers, the phone layout, the unsupported-browser and Brave notes, the bug fix. New tools/check_example_names.py (a fast lint: the made-up data's shape, negative ids, tickers, no test or private name; --live once by hand).
  • Two older checks updated: profile_check now puts back the functions it stands in for before drawing the card again (the example builds its rows with the real itNearList), and intel_place_check leaves the "See an example" line out of the info panel's word count (it is a control, not text to read).
