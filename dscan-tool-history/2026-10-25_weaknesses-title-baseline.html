Fix: "Threat" / "Your" sat above the line of "Weaknesses"

Fix 2026-10-25 · v2026.10.14.58 → v2026.10.14.59.

The owner (2026-10-25, a screenshot of "YOUR Weaknesses" on the open card): "alignment issue".

Cause

The card's title is a button laid out as a flex row, with each item centred (align-items: center). In v.58 "Threat" / "Your" (small capitals) and "Weaknesses" (13 px) became two items of that row, each centred on its own. Their baselines didn't line up: the small word sat 1.5 px high. The collapsed row was fine, because there both words were inline in one span.

Fix

On the open card the word and the name now sit in one inline span (.cc-name), as in the collapsed row, so they share a baseline. The name itself didn't move (its baseline is where it was), and the button's height and the chevron are unchanged. Presentation only.

Checks

  • ttk_why_check.py now measures both baselines on the open card (threat view and My ship view), the collapsed card and Viewing My Ship. The method works for flex and inline layout alike: each text's box less its font's descent. v.58 fails 3 (1.5 px); v.59 passes.
  • Regression suite, pyfa update-check chain and every page check: see the delivery note.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #55: flex items are centred, not set on a baseline. Words meant to read as one line belong in one inline box. To check alignment, compare baselines, not boxes. A 0x0 probe placed after a text gives its baseline only in inline layout; next to a flex item it becomes a flex item and reports the centre. That was this check's first, misleading, reading.
