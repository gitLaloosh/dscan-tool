Answers to the mockup: "A: chip by My Ship", the zKillboard evidence in step 1, both coalition helpers ("Paste a list of alliances", "Suggested friendlies"), details "Both fine".

What you get

  • The D-Scan tab is now the System Analyzer (still last): a Local box and a D-Scan box side by side, one Analyze. With only a scan pasted it shows the same contacts as before, under On Scan. A scan or a fit pasted into the Local box is refused (SYS-02).
  • Your Side, a chip beside My Ship on every tab. Its panel has your pilot (sets your corporation and alliance, looked up again on every Analyze), Also friendly and Hostile lists (one name at a time, or Paste a list: one name per line, tickers ignored), Suggested friendlies (the groups that shot alongside your side on 2+ of its last 50 kills in 7 days, from zKillboard, added with one click), and Pilot rows up to (15, at most 30). Saved in this browser only. On a phone the chip shows your ticker and the panel is a sheet.
  • Local, sorted: the total, a bar and counts for Hostile / Neutral / Friendly, "Fleet" or "Small gang" by the threshold, and names ESI doesn't know. Hostile and Neutral columns, by alliance then corporation, largest first; each group opens to its corporations and up to 6 names each ("+N more"); each name opens the Pilot Analyzer. NPC corporations are one neutral group; with more than 6 neutral groups, the 1–2 pilot groups share a row. Friendly is one closed line.
  • Why hostile: "Your hostile list", or "Killed N of yours in 7 days": zKillboard's list of your alliance's losses in the past 7 days and the newest 50 of those killmails; every attacking pilot's alliance (or corporation) counts once per loss; NPCs and friendlies never. A corporation entry beats its alliance's (a hostile corp inside a friendly alliance is hostile).
  • Pilot Analyzer: a Friendly or Hostile tag beside the pilot's name once Your Side is set.
  • zKillboard, politely (plan D4), for the whole page: one request at a time, 1 s apart; the same request at once is asked once; at most 100 per 10 minutes from a browser (ZKB-04 after that); the pause after a zKillboard failure doubles from 60 s up to 10 minutes. The System Analyzer's zKillboard answers are kept an hour (Refresh skips that), killmails forever, tickers a week.
  • No number the engine works out changed.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #73: what's inside a closed <details> still has a layout box in Chromium (its content is content-visibility: hidden). The 390 px "nothing off the page" check flagged the close buttons of three closed info panels until it skipped what isn't shown with el.checkVisibility(); innerText of an element that isn't rendered likewise returns its raw text. In a check about what someone sees, filter with checkVisibility(), not a zero width.

Also: a request answered once for several callers (the zKillboard queue merging identical requests) must hand each caller its own Response.clone(): a body can be read only once.
