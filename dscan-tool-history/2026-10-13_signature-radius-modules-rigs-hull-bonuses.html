Left out, and new findings

  • The Signature Suppressor's active burst (×0.3) and the MJD's sig spike are short "while active" effects. They belong to the module-toggle item.
  • Found while reading the Defense Mode items: other Defense Mode effects aren't modeled yet. Hecate armor rep cycle ÷1.5, Jackdaw/Skua shield recharge, Confessor/Svipul remote rep. The Skua isn't in the T3D mode table at all.
  • Implant sig effects (Halo set, etc.) are part of the implants item.

Lesson for future models

Two mistakes were behind this batch.

  • The sig drawback was gated on the Rig Shield group, but the same effect sits on 16 Navigation rigs. Gate on the effect, not the group (LESSONS_LEARNED.md #2).
  • Only Interceptors' MWD bloom bonus was entered by hand, while 27 more hulls carry one (#4).
