Drones in damage application: pyfa's getDroneMult

EnginePYFA CORE 2026-10-23 · v2026.10.14.39 → v2026.10.14.40 · This is the first step of My Ship phase 2. the owner's decisions for the matchup strip: split it (strip, tags and amber first), "you last" by damage type with reps, port drone application first, and follow the damage card's settings. For a drone ship like the Vexor, drones are most of its DPS, so the strip would mislead without this.

What changed

  • PYFA CORE application now has pyfa's drone loop and getDroneMult, ported line for line from graphs/data/fitDamageStats/calc/application.py:
    • nothing hits beyond the drone control range (graph setting ignoreDCR, off by default) or, when respected, the lock range;
    • a drone faster than the target hits fully (mobileDroneMode auto, pyfa's default);
    • otherwise it tracks like a turret from its ship's centre, with its own optimal, falloff, tracking and signature resolution, at the drone-surface distance.

Lesson for future models

Before building a comparison feature on top of an engine, check what the engine leaves out for the inputs that matter. Here that was drones, which carry most of the DPS on a drone ship. A headline that silently drops them is worse than no headline. Port the missing piece first, and check it against pyfa with a grid that also varies the settings the new code reads (here the drone control range and the drone mode). A grid that only varies distance and speed would not test those branches.
