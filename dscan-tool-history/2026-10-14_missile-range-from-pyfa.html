Missile range from pyfa's own function

Bug fixPYFA COREUITooling 2026-10-14 · v2026.10.14.6 → v2026.10.14.7 · Erik: "should we make a change to missile range to reflect that of pyfa? if you think so, let's go ahead and do it"

Problem

The tool showed a missile's range as velocity × flight time. pyfa's range column comes from Module.missileMaxRangeData (eos/saveddata/module.py), which:

  • adds the ship's radius to the flight time (pyfa issue #2083), cleaned with floatUnerr
  • flies whole seconds: it computes the range for the second below and the second above, plus the chance of the longer one (pyfa's tooltip), and shows their weighted average (Module.maxRange)
  • counts the first mass × agility / 106 seconds at half speed (acceleration)
  • caps auto-targeting (FoF) missiles at maxFOFTargetRange
  • measures from the ship's surface (subtracts the radius)

The weighted average is usually within metres of velocity × flight time, because the radius is added and then subtracted. It differs where acceleration matters (heavy assault missiles −66 m, XL torpedoes −580 m) and a lot for FoF missiles (Raven auto-targeting cruise missile 222.1 → 199.8 km). This is the third time LESSON #20 applied: the v.17 range batch followed every handler on velocity and flight time, but not the property that turns them into a range.

What changed

  • New PYFA CORE section missile-range: pyfaFloatUnerr and pyfaMissileMaxRangeData, ported line for line. Its inputs are the tool's modified velocity and flight time, the ship's radius (attr 162), the charge's mass and agility (no pyfa handler modifies these), and the FoF cap when the charge has effect 104. The SDE default of 200 km applies when the cap attribute is absent.
  • Result: travelDistanceM is now pyfa's displayed range. New fields travelLowerM, travelHigherM and travelHigherChancePct hold the two whole-second outcomes.
  • UI: hovering the missile range in the Firepower card shows pyfa's tooltip, e.g. "92% chance to fly 1085.4 km, 8% chance to fly 1090.1 km". The methodology note was updated.
  • Tooling:
    • tools/independent_checks/check_missile_range.py (new) lifts pyfa's function unmodified from module.py (ast) and runs it with pyfa's own floatUnerr on oracle attributes. It is in the chain, which now has 14 steps.
    • compare_modes.py uses the same function.
    • golden_oracle.py now defaults the Anhinga to its first mode, as the tool does.
    • run_regression.mjs flattens the split fields.
  • DEVIATIONS.md: the missile-range entry is removed.

Lesson for future models

  • LESSON #20, third time: following every handler on an attribute is not enough. Read the pyfa property that consumes it (missileMaxRangeData, rawCycleTime, getCycleParameters).
  • When a pyfa function is self-contained, lift it out of pyfa's source and run it as the reference (ast + its own helpers) rather than re-deriving it (LESSON #3).
