Fix: the Matchup's info panel was cut off at the board's edge

Fix 2026-10-24 · v2026.10.14.51 → v2026.10.14.52.

The owner (2026-10-24, screenshot of a Pilot loss, Loki vs his Osprey Navy Issue): "info panel clipping bottom edge of the matchup bar". The "How Matchup works" panel (new in v.51) showed only its title and first line; the rest was cut off where the board ends.

Cause

  • Wide layouts: the board has overflow: hidden, so its colored halves follow its rounded corners. The ⓘ button sits in the board's middle column, which was position: relative. That made the middle column the panel's frame, inside the board, so the board clipped the panel at its bottom edge.
  • Below 900 px: the button's wrapper was centered with a transform. A transform makes an element the frame even for a position: fixed child, so on a phone the panel's bottom sheet was squeezed into the 34 px button wrapper instead of filling the screen's width.

No check had opened this panel. layout_check.py opens every card's panel, but only with no ship saved, when there's no Matchup.

Fix

  • The board no longer clips its contents. The two halves round their own top corners, so the board looks the same.
  • The button's wrapper is centered with flex instead of a transform.

Presentation only; no calculation changed.

Checks

  • fight_strip_check.py opens the Matchup's panel at 1440, 1100 and 375 px. Every corner and the middle of its bottom edge must be the panel itself on screen (document.elementFromPoint), with its text inside. v.51 fails at all three widths (3, 3 and 2 corners not seen; a 34 px sheet on the phone); v.52 passes.
  • layout_check.py: the same corner test for every card's panel.
  • Regression suite ALL PASS; update-check chain all 36 steps OK; every page check 0 failures.
  • layout_check.py has stopped twice since v.51 with "Execution context was destroyed", after a Pilot tab click, with no failed check before it. A run with page-navigation logging showed no stray navigation, and reruns pass. It is logged to watch, not hidden with a retry.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #46, again: a pop-up checked in one place can break in another. This time it wasn't off the screen but cut off by an ancestor's overflow: hidden, or trapped by an ancestor's transform. When a pop-up goes into a new container, check that each open panel is actually seen: elementFromPoint at its corners must return the panel. Look for overflow and transform on every ancestor.
