Your profile

UIIntelMy Fits 2026-11-01 · v2026.11.01.1

Why

The owner (2026-10-29): "turn the upper right portion of the page into something of a 'profile' of the user that starts out as being incomplete or not started. similar to a profile for a discord account. encompasses all things associated with the user (and later tied into Eve SSO). Pilot name, associations, fits, etc. Intel could sit outside of that but somehow attached as a decorator." Four mockup passes followed on 10-29; the fifth (2026-10-31, on today's page) settled the rest: the status colours for Intel's mark, the panel with 20 fits, the close button, and the Intel card's info button. Bundled at the owner's choice: the status-colour step, the My Ships panel running past the window (a bug), the close button for these panels (a bug, in part), and Intel's info button (a bug).

What changed

  • One profile control at the top right replaces the Standings chip, the My Ship chip, "Use in assessments" and the "N near" pill: your portrait (initials when it can't load), name and tickers; "Set up your profile" and "n of 3" until your pilot, Standings and a fit are set.
  • "Use in assessments" is one wide switch with the fit in use inside it; with no fit, "+ Add a fit", which opens the profile at the paste box.
  • The panel: Pilot (with "Log in with EVE" waiting for SSO), Standings and My Fits, each ticked when done. It has the shared close button at its top right; Esc and a press outside close it too, and the focus goes back to the profile. It never runs past the bottom of the window: it scrolls inside, and the wheel isn't trapped. On a phone it's a sheet from the bottom.
  • "My Ship" reads "My Fit" everywhere users read it: My Fits, the My Fit Perspective, Viewing My Fit, "Set as my fit", the error texts (ERRORS.md regenerated) and roadmap/card-names.md.
  • Intel's mark on the profile: off none; on and clear a cyan square on the portrait's corner; on but not working (an error, or the kill feed failing or stopped) a hollow cyan square; near an amber "Intel · N near" tab on the profile's left; in system the Hostile standing's red, "IN SYSTEM", with a ring that pulses (none with reduced motion). The Intel tab carries a small dot of the same kind. The colours are tokens (--st-clear, --st-near, --st-in); the three sets offered were checked with the colour-blind validator, and green for "clear" was turned down because green and amber are 3.8 apart for red-green colour blindness (the floor is 8).
  • The Intel card's head: the switch, the gear, then the info button, at the top right at every width (in windows 900 px wide and under they used to move beside the title). A closed info panel now takes no room (Chrome laid out its content at the card's right edge, past a phone's window).

Lesson for future models

LESSON #117: a button laid under other controls has to be pressed where nothing sits on top. The profile is one big button with the switch and "+ Add a fit" on top of it; a click aimed at its middle lands on "+ Add a fit".
