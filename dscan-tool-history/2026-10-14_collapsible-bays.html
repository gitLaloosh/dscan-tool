Collapsible bays in the Fit card

Layout 2026-10-14 · v2026.10.14.18 → v2026.10.14.19 · the owner: "can we please make any bays in the fit collapsible as well? drone bay, cargo bay, etc."

What changed

  • The Fit card's Drone bay, Fighter bay, Fleet hangar and Cargo headings are now toggles, like the card titles since v.18 (chevron before the heading). A closed bay is one row with what it holds:
    • Drone bay: drones in space and what's in the bay, e.g. "5 / 5 in space · 5 types · 12 items".
    • Other bays: "2 types · 60 items".
  • Same mechanism as the cards: cardIsOpen() / f.cardOpen (keys bay:drone, bay:fighter, bay:fleet, bay:cargo) and wireCardToggles(), so a bay's open/closed state survives mode, module-state and drone changes, is kept per fit (each Pilot Analyzer loss separately), and focus stays on the toggle.
  • Defaults: all open on desktop and tablet; on a phone the cargo and fleet hangar start closed (long lists, rarely needed for a fight) and the drone and fighter bays open.
  • Works in both the Ring and List views (the bays are listed under either). Implants and Boosters are not bays and stay as they are. Presentation only.

Lesson for future models

  • Reuse the existing toggle mechanism for a new collapsible section rather than adding a second one; then every re-render path already keeps its state. When a check counts "all toggles", narrow it when a new kind of toggle is added (here the card checks had to exclude the bay toggles).
