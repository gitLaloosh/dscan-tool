Intel: kills after a long gap

FixTests 2026-10-29 · v2026.10.29.3 → v2026.10.29.4

The owner, on v2026.10.29.3 with the kill range at 10: "I should note that i still have not seen a single killmail show up in the feed." Kills 6 and 9 jumps away that RIFT showed were missing from the Feed. He asked whether this could be tested over a period of time instead of in one sitting.

How it was found

  • A recording, not a probe. The page's own kill reader, unchanged, ran in the built-in browser pane from 10:04 to 10:47 UTC. It was slowed to at most 3 requests a second (1,879 requests, no error from zKillboard) and logged every request and every kill with why it was kept or dropped.
  • Finding the newest kill worked (9 s), and so did the read-back. zKillboard had kills 1 to 3 minutes after they happened. Distances from the page's own jump table matched RIFT's.
  • At 10:05, numbers 100274284 to 100274287 were missing, and still were 40 minutes later, while 100274288 onward were there.
  • v.103 looks only 3 numbers past a missing one. Four missing in a row read as "nothing new yet", so the page asked for the same four every 8 s and read no kill after 10:05. The owner then sent a kill from RIFT (10:41, TZN-2V, 9 jumps). The log showed it was never asked for.
  • The page's "far behind" check runs only after a kill is read, so it never ran.
  • Restarting Intel finds the newest kill again, which is why kills sometimes appeared for a while.

What you get

  • No kill read for a minute: the page looks further ahead. It probes 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, then 12, 16, 24 ... up to 2048 numbers past where it is (at most 16 requests, at most once a minute). When it finds a kill, it finds the newest from there and reads forward to it.
  • A gap is not the end. Missing numbers before a kill known further on are read past, even a gap wider than one read-back batch of 28.
  • Missing numbers are still asked for again (at 6 s, 30 s, 2 min and 5 min), as in v.103, but at most 40 of them, so a long gap isn't asked for 4 times over.
  • The Intel status line says whether kills are coming in: "last kill read N s ago", "kills: catching up" while reading back, and "no kill read for N min" in amber after 5 minutes without one. A stuck reader no longer looks like a quiet night. The info panel says so too.
  • No change to how often the page asks while kills arrive. Every request still goes through the one gate (at most 10 a second).
  • No number the engine works out changed.

Checks

  • intel_kills_check.py, a new part:
    • Four numbers missing for good, then kills, as recorded. After a minute with no kill read (set, not waited), the kill 7 jumps away shows within seconds and the page is at the newest.
    • Then 40 missing in a row, wider than a read-back batch: read past, the kill shown, at most 40 numbers kept to ask again.
    • At most 12 requests in any second throughout.
    • The status line's "last kill read N s ago", and "no kill read for 6 min" in amber.
    v2026.10.29.3 fails: it stays at the gap (5007 while kills run to 5016).
  • Four broken versions, each caught: no look-ahead after a minute, misses before a known kill taken as the end, no cap on the numbers kept, no status.
  • Regression suite ALL PASS (269 fits). Update-check chain: 45 steps, all OK. All 45 page checks pass. The lints, the personal-data check and the request-gate check report 0 problems.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #98: a reader that waits for data must notice when it has waited too long. Don't size a look-ahead from the gaps you've seen; give it a time limit, and make every recovery path run whether or not data arrived. Show the reader's health where the user looks. When a bug comes and goes, record the real thing over time, paced well under the service's limit, rather than probing once from a fresh start.
