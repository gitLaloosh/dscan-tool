Last kill details in a panel that floats over the page

Layout 2026-10-23 · v2026.10.14.43 → v2026.10.14.44.

The owner's screenshot of v.43 showed a problem: opening the last kill made its details expand inside the header line. The pilot name and hull dropdown slid down to the middle of a tall block, and the loss box was pushed far down the page. Option A of the "Last kill details" mockup (My Ship canvas) was chosen: "I like A".

What changed

  • A panel under the "Last kill" line. It floats over the page, so the header and the loss box never move. It is shifted left when it would run off the right edge. Escape, a press outside it, or the pivot button close it.
  • Inside the panel:
    • "Vedmak killed a Curse" with the date and time;
    • the pilot's own weapon with its damage tag (left out when zKillboard only names the hull as the weapon);
    • "See <pilot>'s Vedmak losses" and "view on zKillboard";
    • the other attackers as a table: pilot, hull, weapon and damage tag (the weapon's damage types, or "ammo" when it depends on the ammo).
    In the table, the final blow comes first, then the attackers by damage done. Five rows show, then "… N more". The weapon reads "—" when zKillboard names no weapon or only the hull. The attackers are looked up once, the first time the panel opens. The old "Show / hide other attackers" button is gone.
  • Phone: the panel is a sheet across the bottom of the screen with a close button. Each attacker takes two lines.
  • The hull dropdown also closes on Escape or a press outside it. One listener pair serves the whole page, so re-analyzing doesn't pile up listeners.

Verification

  • New tools/page_checks/last_kill_check.py: 24 checks, 0 failures. zKillboard and ESI are mocked, with a last kill that has seven other attackers. It checks:
    • the header doesn't move and the panel floats over the loss box;
    • the headline, time, weapon tag and both actions;
    • the attacker order, the "—" weapons, the damage tags, and "… 2 more" and "Show fewer";
    • Escape, a press outside and the pivot button;
    • at 1000 and 700 px the panel stays on screen;
    • the phone sheet.
  • busy_check.py: the header checks pass with the panel.
  • Everything else: all other page checks pass, the regression suite passes and the 34-step update check passes. No calculation changed.

Lesson for future models

A detail view that opens inside a flex row grows the whole row. That is how the header line ended up as tall as the attacker list. Details that can be long belong in a layer over the page (a panel, or a sheet on a phone), anchored to what opened them. Test the open state at a narrow width too, not only the closed one.
