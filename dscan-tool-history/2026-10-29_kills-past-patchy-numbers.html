Intel: kills past patchy numbers

FixTests 2026-10-29 · v2026.10.29.4 → v2026.10.29.5

The owner, the same afternoon as v.29.4: "it looks like that last kill feed fix didn't work." The status line said "no kill read for 15 min" while RIFT showed kills.

How it was found

  • The page's own state, read in the owner's browser console with a one-line snippet, twice 20 s apart: waiting at number 100275281, not busy, no error, still making requests, and the minute-check had just run.
  • Probed by hand from the browser pane: 100275281 to 100275299 missing, 100275300 there, then missing numbers between kills (305, 313, 320, 329, 345, 409 ...). His console showed the same numbers answering 404.
  • v.29.4's minute-check looked at fixed numbers ahead: 4 to 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and so on. Every one of those was a missing number, so it found nothing and tried again a minute later, with the same result.

What you get

  • After 30 s with no kill (was a minute), the page reads forward one number after another until 32 in a row are missing past the last kill it found. Any gap under 32 is crossed, whatever its pattern. At most 400 numbers per look; if it is still finding kills at 400, it carries on at once.
  • Only then does it look further: 8 numbers in a row at 32, 64, 128 ... 2048 past, instead of single numbers.
  • Starting (or restarting) Intel no longer stops at a gap. Finding the newest kill used to look only 3 past where it stopped; the owner's restart landed on the same 100275281 (his console: the two kills never read). It now looks up to 32 on, skipping numbers found missing in the last 6 s.
  • A quiet feed isn't hammered. Finding nothing anywhere, it waits twice as long before the next look (30 s, then 1, 2, 4 and 5 minutes); a kill found resets it to 30 s. One look costs at most 88 requests, all through the one gate (at most 10 a second).
  • The status line and the info panel say the page keeps reading ahead past missing numbers.
  • No number the engine works out changed.

Checks

  • intel_kills_check.py, a new part with the pattern from the owner's feed: 19 missing after where the page is, and missing numbers at every spot v.29.4 looked at, with kills between them. After 30 s with no kill (set, not waited) the kill shows and the page reaches the newest. Then nothing new anywhere: one look costs at most 32 + 56 requests, and the next look comes a minute later. v2026.10.29.4 fails.
  • A start with 19 missing in a row near the newest: the newest is found past them (v.29.4's look of 3 stops before them).
  • R2Z2 requests per second are now timed in the page itself (the test's own timing could bunch under load): at most 10 to 12 in any second throughout.
  • Three broken versions, each caught: no reading forward, no longer waits after finding nothing, finding the newest looking only 3 on.
  • Regression suite, update-check chain, page checks, lints: see the jump-table history doc for this delivery.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #99: a look-ahead at fixed spots can miss every time. To cross a gap of unknown size and pattern, read through it until a run of misses long enough to mean "the end", then probe further with windows, and back off when nothing is found. When a user says a fix didn't work, ask for the program's own state first; a one-line console snippet was enough here.
