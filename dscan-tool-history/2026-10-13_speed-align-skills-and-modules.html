What changed

Everything here is grounded in pyfa's own effect handlers (eos/effects.py) and its stacking rules (eos/modifiedAttributeDict.py), using CCP's SDE values from pyfa's local static-data cache. pyfa's rule is that, per attribute, all stacking-penalized multipliers share one penalty group, split into a "bigger than 1" chain and a "smaller than 1" chain that are penalized independently. Skills are never penalized.

Bug fixed along the way. The armor-rig agility drawback used to be gated on the whole "Rig Armor" group. That wrongly applied +5% agility for each Auxiliary Nano Pump and Nanobot Accelerator, whose drawback attribute is actually a powergrid penalty. The gate is now the rig's own drawbackAgility effect (2717). The Muninn golden fit carried two Nano Pumps and was reading 7.2 s instead of 5.9 s.

Mass ordering now matches pyfa. Plates and an active prop mod's mass are added first, then multipliers (Higgs, siege/core ×10) apply to the total. An active prop mod's own speed boost stays a separate multiplier, because pyfa puts it in its own postMul penalty group, not the module chain.

Each moved by exactly ×0.90 (rounding aside), or ×0.506 on capitals, unless a speed or agility module was fitted. Every module-driven change was hand-checked: Rifter, Osprey Navy Issue, Vedmak, Confessor, Muninn.
