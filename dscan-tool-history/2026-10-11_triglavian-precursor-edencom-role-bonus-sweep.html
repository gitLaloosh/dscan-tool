Triglavian/Precursor + EDENCOM hull SHIP_ROLE_BONUS sweep (independent review finding #11)

2026-10-11 · dscan-tool.html

The problem

SHIP_ROLE_BONUS (turret/missile/drone damage+rate-of-fire hull bonuses) had only ever been built for the Triglavian/Precursor weapon family reactively, hull-by-hull, across separate historical bug-fix sessions: Vedmak and Leshak (2026-10-09), Drekavac (an earlier pass). It was never run as a full exhaustive sweep the way SHIP_EWAR_BONUS's non-Recon pass and SHIP_RESIST_BONUS's generic pass both were on 2026-10-11 earlier today. This finding closes that gap by running the identical methodology against the full Triglavian/EDENCOM hull roster.

Method

  1. Live ESI sweep: /universe/categories/6 → all 50 ship groups → all 424 published hull types → all 1,350 unique dogma_effects, filtered for any shipID-domain effect targeting attribute 64 (damageMultiplier) or 51 (rate of fire).
  2. Cross-validated every hit against everef.net's Traits JSON: a bonus listed under role_bonuses is flat/unscaled; one listed under types[skillId] is per-level, multiplied by 5 at this project's assumed skill-V convention.
  3. Confirmed the complete 14-hull Triglavian/EDENCOM roster by direct name lookup against the 424-hull set: Damavik, Draugur, Drekavac, Ikitursa, Kikimora, Leshak, Nergal, Rodiva, Skybreaker, Stormbringer, Tiamat, Vedmak, Zarmazd, Zirnitra.
  4. For every candidate, read the hull's own live dogma_attributes directly (never assumed from a sister hull) to get the exact per-level magnitude.
  5. Built isolating golden fits for each new magnitude/category, verified against the tool's own sandboxed estimateHpAndDps() output before locking in expected values.

Confirmed non-gaps

Rodiva (49712) — the Collective's own predecessor support-cruiser to Zarmazd (per its in-game description). Its full dogma_effects list is 100% Mutadaptive Remote Armor Repairer / Remote Capacitor Transfer / cap-need-reduction related — no disintegrator or vorton weapon effect, no resist effect, at all. Correctly gets no entry in either table, same class of finding as Zarmazd (already confirmed in finding #5).
Vedmak, Drekavac, Leshak — already correct from earlier sessions; re-verified unchanged.

Explicitly out of scope (not modeled anywhere in this tool, not unique to Triglavian hulls)

Hull-level weapon range/falloff role bonuses. Vedmak's and Drekavac's 2nd Type Bonus (optimal range), Kikimora's and Draugur's range-only role+skill bonuses, and Stormbringer's Vorton range bonus are all real, live-ESI-confirmed bonuses — but this tool has no hull-weapon-range-bonus mechanic for any hull in any faction (confirmed: grepped the whole file for a hull-level turret/launcher range-bonus consumer and found none). This is a pre-existing, tool-wide gap, not something unique to the Triglavian line, so it's left for a future dedicated finding rather than bolted on here as a one-off.
Tiamat's turret tracking-speed self-buff (effect 7086, +25% at V) — no turret-tracking-vs-target-speed simulation exists in this tool at all.
Tiamat's Recon-ship-specific "additional bonus to damage multiplier per cycle" (effect 7119, a runtime stacking mechanic that ramps damage the longer the weapon stays on target) — a pure runtime ship-state mechanic with no fitted item to leave a trace on a D-scan/killmail snapshot, same class as the Tactical Destroyer Mode mechanic already documented elsewhere in this file.
Nergal's and Ikitursa's generic Assault-Frigate/HAC-class skill bonus (range + "maximum damage multiplier bonus" tied to the Assault Damage Control module's cap) — a universal class-level mechanic shared by every faction's assault frigate/HAC, not Triglavian-specific, and not modeled for any hull (no ADC-cap simulation exists).
