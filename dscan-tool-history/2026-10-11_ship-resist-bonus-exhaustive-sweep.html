Hull resist bonuses: 6 hand-picked hulls → 90, from an exhaustive live sweep

Context: independent review finding #5 (deferred to the backlog earlier today), picked by Erik to resolve next. The old SHIP_RESIST_BONUS table held 6 hand-picked hulls (Muninn, Nighthawk, Sacrilege, Broadsword, Absolution, Rokh). The reviewer listed 9 more it had spotted by reading trait pages; rather than patching those 9, the table was regenerated from an exhaustive sweep so it can't silently miss hulls again.

Root cause

The table was built on 2026-10-08 from whichever hulls happened to come up during a role-bonus re-audit, not from a sweep. Every hull with an intrinsic all-shield/all-armor resist bonus that wasn't one of those 6 had its tanked layer's resonance missing a ×0.8 (or ×0.75/×0.7) factor — about 20% too little EHP on that layer for 84 hulls, including very common ones: Drake, Maller, Moa, Abaddon, Prophecy, Punisher, Merlin, Harpy, Eagle, Vulture, Damnation, Claymore, Eos, every HIC except the Broadsword, and every carrier/supercarrier/FAX/dread with a resist bonus. Drekavac's armor-resist bonus was even noted in a SHIP_ROLE_BONUS comment but never added.

Method (so this can be re-run after a balance patch, not hand-patched)

  1. ESI /universe/categories/6 → all 50 ship groups → all 424 published hull types.
  2. Every hull's dogma_effects (1,350 unique effects) fetched from /dogma/effects/{id}.
  3. Kept every effect with a modifier targeting a resonance attribute: armor 267–270, shield 271–274, hull 109/110/111/113. Every hit had the same shape — ItemModifier, domain shipID, operator 6 (PostPercent), driven by a hull attribute holding the (negative) magnitude — and every hit covered all 4 damage types with one shared number, so a uniform per-layer value is exact.
  4. All 90 hits cross-checked line-by-line against CCP's SDE trait data (ref-data.everef.net/types/{id} → traits) to tell per-skill-level bonuses (×5 at the max-level-V convention) from flat role bonuses — the raw dogma magnitude alone can't distinguish them. 90/90 ESI-vs-trait magnitudes agree exactly; all 6 pre-existing entries re-confirmed unchanged.

New capability: hull (structure) resist bonus

Two hulls carry a hull-resist bonus, which the table never supported: Monitor (Flag Cruiser, 4%/lvl to shield+armor+hull) and Hubris (Lancer Dreadnought, 5%/lvl hull only). adjHullRes now reads a hull key, same multiplicative slot as the existing shield/armor ones.

Also corrected

The Fit Analyzer methodology note said "a handful of hulls carry their own intrinsic … resistance skill bonus", implying a complete, small list. Updated to say about 90 hulls, from an exhaustive sweep of live game data, including flat role bonuses and the rare hull-layer case. A stale code comment about Archon/Chimera ("not yet added there either") updated.

Verification

Two new isolating golden fits, both hand-derived and exact-matched to the sandbox:

  • drake_constructed_hull_shield_resist_bonus — bare Drake: shield EHP 5500×1.25/(0.725×0.8) = 11,853 (was 9,482); armor 6,019 and hull 6,996 unchanged (negative-path guard). Resulting shield resists 20/36/52/60% (EM/Th/Kin/Exp) — the well-known bare-Drake-at-Battlecruiser-V profile.
  • monitor_constructed_hull_resist_bonus_all_layers — bare Monitor: shield and armor EHP 140,625 each, hull EHP 4,664 (was 112,500/112,500/3,731), exercising the new hull-layer path.

Full suite: 54/54 PASS. No existing golden fit changed — the only two real fits on newly-added hulls (Abaddon, Stormbringer) assert DPS/weapon counts only, not EHP. Not pyfa-screenshot-confirmed; an optional quick check would be a bare Drake in pyfa (should show the same 20/36/52/60 shield profile).

Not in scope / follow-ups noted

Unpublished hulls were excluded (can't normally appear on a killmail). T3 cruiser Defensive subsystems that grant resists are a separate table/mechanism and weren't part of this sweep. Tactical Destroyer Defense Mode resists are handled by their own table (and are backlog item #9).
