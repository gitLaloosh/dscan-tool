Lesson for future models

LESSONS #46 and #47.

  • Check a shared piece everywhere it goes. The info panel was checked on the one card it was built for. On a card in the narrow left column it would have opened off the screen. When a panel, menu or tooltip goes onto more cards, open every one at every layout width. Place it against something that can't be narrower than it.
  • Copy anchors, don't retype them. A big replace script stopped at its first anchor, which was typed from memory with one space too many, and nothing was written. Read the exact text first, and split a big change into small patches that each check and save on their own.
