Long lists stay in their box

Page 2026-11-07 · v2026.11.07.5

Why

  • Build queue 011, Project doc roadmap/list-caps-plan.md, mockup approved from list_caps_mockup.html (the owner: "look good"). With a big Local, the System Analyzer's columns, an opened alliance's corporations, the Ships on Scan rail and a dossier's kill rows pushed the rest of the page far down.
  • The owner liked the amber buttons ("i like the amber"), and folded in the bug that the ▲/▼ kills and losses on corporation lines started right after each name, so they didn't line up ("sure").

What changed

  • Hostile and Neutral columns (over 760 px): each no taller than the window less 24 px, scrolling inside; the heading stays at the top; a "+N more groups below" line at the bottom while groups are hidden there. Opening a group scrolls its column to show it, never the page. A redraw (a change to Standings, the kills arriving) keeps each column's scroll and the focus. The wheel at a column's end goes on to the page.
  • An opened group shows its first 8 corporations, then "Show N more corporations" with their pilots counted; pressed, all of them and "Show fewer". Nothing is asked of any server: the data is already in hand.
  • Corporation lines are three columns: the name, the ▲ kills and ▼ losses each in a right-aligned slot of its own, and the pilot count.
  • Ships on Scan (over 760 px, beside Local): capped at the window like the columns, scrolling inside. Its hover card now sits outside the rail so the rail's box can't cut it off; it follows its row as the rail or the page scrolls and closes when the row scrolls out of view.
  • Pilot dossier: past 10 kill rows, the rows scroll inside a box 10 rows tall with the heading row on top; "Show fewer" sits beside "Show 10 more", and after "Show 10 more" the box shows the first new row. Its scroll survives a redraw.
  • Buttons: "Show 10 more", "Show N more hulls" and the new "Show N more corporations" use the page's amber, compact size.
  • 760 px and under: unchanged. Nothing kept in the browser changes.

Checks

  • New list_caps_check.py (made-up data: 80 neutral corporations, a 40-corporation hostile alliance, 30 small hostile alliances, a 46-hull scan, a pilot with 42 kills in one hull): every item above, the alignment measured with 1-, 2- and 3-digit counts, the card on top of Local's cards at its corners, and 760 px uncapped.
  • Two older checks read the new markup: scan_rail_check.py counts the card in #saOut, and dossier_kills_check.py looks for the "Show 10 more" button itself (a "Show fewer" button is there now too).

Lesson for future models

  • LESSON #146: measure rows in a scroll box against the box itself. A sticky heading's offsetTop moves while it is stuck, so a height worked out from it was right at the first draw and 839 px off once the box had scrolled. The box is position:relative now and the measure uses plain rows.
