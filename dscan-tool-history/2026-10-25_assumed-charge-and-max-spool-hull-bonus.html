A weapon with no charge loaded: the charge aboard that's their best against you

FixFeatureTests 2026-10-25 · v2026.10.14.66 → v2026.10.14.67.

The owner reported it through the inbox: "the following fit shows zero dps" (a pasted Nergal). Then: "the zero was on the matchup card." The Nergal's Light Entropic Disintegrator II has no charge loaded, and four charges sit in cargo.

  • pyfa computes a fit as it is given. With no charge, a weapon deals no damage (eos Module.getVolleyParameters reads the damage from the charge).
  • The Matchup therefore said "0 DPS applied to you", "you last: holds" and "edge: you", while Firepower showed a range from the cargo ammo.

The owner's decisions: "Assume a charge", and of which charge, "Their best against you".

What changed

  • Which weapons: a weapon type with no charge in any copy, and with a charge aboard that it loads (pyfa isValidCharge).
  • Where: the Damage Application card and the Matchup.
  • Which charge: the one the Weaknesses picks already rank first at the fight's point, which kills the target fastest.
    • It's ranked by the strip's time to kill against the target's resists, then by its buffer alone.
    • The charge is loaded into every copy and the fit is run through the engine again (wpCandidates). The numbers are pyfa's for that fit.
    • In the My Ship Perspective it is exactly the "What the Nergal should use against you" pick.
  • Shown as:
    • on the card, one amber line "Assumed loaded: Tetryon Exotic Plasma S", with the sentence on hover;
    • on the board, "DPS applied to you" (or "to them" for your own ship) gets a dotted amber underline and the sentence on hover;
    • the "you last" / "they last" why text names the charge.
  • Firepower is unchanged: it still shows the range over every charge aboard.
  • Stable frame: the chosen charge follows the fight point, as the picks do. The range slider's maximum, the default range and the number boxes are worked out from every candidate at once (dmgSpanApp, dmgDefaultsFor), so the card doesn't move when the charge changes.
  • Code: one block, // ==== ASSUMED CHARGES. mysFight's attacker applications and dmgModel pass through dmgAssumeApp.
  • DEVIATIONS.md: a type with some copies loaded and some empty counts only its loaded copies.

The owner's Nergal against his Osprey Navy Issue, at the default 7 km:

  • before: 0 DPS, you hold;
  • now: 506 DPS applied with Tetryon Exotic Plasma S assumed. You last 1 min 23 s; edge: you.

Found on the way: the Nergal's DPS was 40% low

The new check compared the tool with pyfa on the owner's Nergal and found a hull bonus the tool had missed.

  • The bonus: the Nergal, Ikitursa and Babaroga double their disintegrator's maximum spool-up, damageMultiplierBonusMax 2.125 → 4.25 (pyfa Effect7216 / 7211 / 12288).
  • Why it was missed: the hull damage generator only recorded handlers on damageMultiplier and speed. It now records kind 'spool' rows, and hullWeaponMods returns spool, which multiplies the spool-up on both damage paths (the loaded charge and cargo ammo).
  • Effect: Nergal + Occult S went from 382 to 642, exactly pyfa (641.54).
  • PYFA CORE hull-damage was re-verified (generator up to date, check_firepower.py, compare_fit_stats.py 0 mismatches over 258 fits, regression) and re-locked.
  • New golden fits: nergal / ikitursa / babaroga _constructed_max_spool_hull_bonus, with pins from pyfa's side: 641.5 / 1,300.4 / 1,793.0. v.66 fails all 6 pins.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #62: a generated table covers only the attributes you listed.

  • The disintegrator formula reads damageMultiplierBonusMax, but the hull table recorded only damageMultiplier and speed. Three hulls were about 40% low since the disintegrators were added (2026-10-08), and no golden fit used them.
  • For every attribute a formula reads, grep pyfa's handlers and record them (or assert there are none).
  • Add a golden fit for each hull that has a bonus specific to a weapon system.
