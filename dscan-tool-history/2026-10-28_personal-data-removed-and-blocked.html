Lesson for future models

LESSON #90: a public repository keeps everything you save in it, so check every saved file for personal data, pictures included. Each piece here was saved for a good reason: a quote of a decision, a fixture label, a real paste to test the parser. Nothing asked who it pointed at. Make test data up, label fixtures by hull and killmail, never save a screenshot or raw intel, review every embedded picture, and let check_pii.py refuse the rest. Cleaning the files does not clean the history. And when replacing names in bulk, replace only full, distinctive strings. A first pass replaced the single words of scrambled ship names everywhere, which rewrote cargo and tackle in the code; it was caught by reading the diff before anything was delivered.
