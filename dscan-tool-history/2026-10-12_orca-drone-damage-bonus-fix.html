Orca drone-damage role-bonus magnitude fix

2026-10-12 · dscan-tool.html · KNOWN OPEN ITEM #5 in dscan-tool.md: "Orca's 2 drone-damage effects don't fit either convention"

The gap

The original 2026-10-08 ship-role-bonus audit flagged Orca as not fitting the usual "1 flat role bonus + 1 skill-scaled type bonus" pattern cleanly, and the table ended up with [["drone","damage",50],["drone","damage",100]] — neither number traceable to anything in Orca's own data. This session re-derived both numbers from scratch against live dogma.

Root cause, grounded

Fetched Orca's (type 28606) full raw dogma_attributes and dogma_effects live via ref-data.everef.net, then looked up the two relevant attributes/effects by name:

AttributeNameValueEffectSource
2580industrialBonusDroneDamage100.06789 (industrialBonusDroneDamage, modifies attr 64)Flat role bonus, not skill-scaled (everef Traits role_bonuses: "+100% Drone damage")
3203industrialCommandBonusDroneDamage15.08261 (modifies attr 64)Per-level Industrial Command Ships skill (type 29637) bonus (everef Traits types["29637"]: "+15%/lvl Drone hitpoints and damage") → 75% at this file's standing max-skill-V convention

Neither 50 nor 100 (the old entry's magnitudes) matches a real component here except coincidentally reusing the flat bonus's raw number in the wrong slot. The actual pair is 100 (flat) + 75 (skill@V), compounding multiplicatively exactly like every other multi-entry hull in this table (2.0 × 1.75 = 3.5x total), not 1.5 × 2.0 = 3.0x as the old entry gave.

Fix

Changed the table entry from [["drone","damage",50],["drone","damage",100]] to [["drone","damage",100],["drone","damage",75]]. No new mechanism needed — this hull always fit the tool's existing "flat + skill-scaled, compound multiplicatively" convention, it just had the wrong two numbers in it.

Verification

New golden fit orca_constructed_industrial_command_drone_bonus_fix (bare Orca hull + 10x Hobgoblin II in the drone bay, no other modules), sandbox-verified against the live post-fix code:

Drone DPS (5 deployed)297347

Hand-derivation: dmgPerCycle = 20(thermal) × 1.92(Hobgoblin II's own multiplier) × 2.0625(Drone Interfacing × Light Drone Operation × Gallente Drone Specialization, all V) × 3.5(this fix) = 277.2; perDroneDps = 277.2/4s = 69.3; droneDps = 69.3 × 5 deployed = 346.5, rounds to 347 — exact match to sandbox output.

Unlike the Griffin NI malus fix earlier today, this hull was being undercounted — the old wrong entry (3.0x) understated real drone DPS (3.5x) by about 14% (297 vs. the correct 347). Worth flagging since threat-assessment tools that undercount are arguably more dangerous than ones that overcount.

Scope

Only drone DAMAGE is covered by this fix — the same effect/skill combo also grants +15%/lvl Drone HITPOINTS (bundled into the same real-game bonus), but this tool has never tracked drone HP for any ship (confirmed via code search, zero references), so that half remains out of scope, same as it is for every other hull. Orca's many other role/skill bonuses (mining yield, ice harvesting, Command Burst range/strength, tractor beam, cargo/ore hold, jump fatigue) are all unrelated to threat-assessment DPS/EHP and were not touched.
