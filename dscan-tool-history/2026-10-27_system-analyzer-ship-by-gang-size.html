Delivery 2 of roadmap/pilot-dossier-plan.md (D4–D6). The owner on the dossier mockup: "for #4 that's fine but give users the option to switch between group sizes", then "One switch, Auto default" and "Their alliance"; "ok, proceed with delivery 2".

What you get

  • A switch on the Pilots card: Auto / Solo / Small gang / Fleet / All. Auto is the default; your choice is kept in this browser.
  • Auto picks each pilot's size from how many of their alliance (or corporation, when they have none) are in Local: 1 solo, 2–10 small gang, 11+ fleet. A pilot in an NPC corporation counts as solo. The other settings use one size for every row.
  • The possible ship is the hull with the most of their kills in that size, with a tag naming the size (in the dossier's colours) and "12 of 20 small-gang kills · 28 h ago" under it. The whole sentence is on hover, including why that size ("Small gang: 2 of Random Third Party in Local."). All counts kills and losses together, without a tag.
  • Unchanged:
    • A ship of theirs from the last 30 days that's on your D-Scan still comes first.
    • No kill in that size: v.84's rule (the newest ship seen within 30 days, else the hull they lost most), with the reason on hover.
    • The numbers come from their newest loss in that hull, else "fit unknown".
  • Opening a row brings up the Pilot Analyzer with the dossier on the same size and that hull selected.
  • Requests: still 2 zKillboard requests per pilot (their kills and losses lists). Each row now reads their newest 50 kills and 30 losses from ESI (it was 5 and 10), the dossier's own numbers; killmails are kept for good, so only a pilot's first look is slower. Switching sizes asks nothing.
  • No number the engine works out changed.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #80: a check that still passes after a change may never reach the change. Three checks passed at once, untouched; their data took the old branch every time. When old checks pass after a behaviour change, find out which branch their data takes before counting them as evidence, and write a check whose data reaches the new path.
