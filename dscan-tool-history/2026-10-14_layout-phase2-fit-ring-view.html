Layout Phase 2: the fit ring and the Ring / List switch

LayoutFeature 2026-10-14 · v2026.10.14.15 → v2026.10.14.16 · Erik: "both with a switch like workbench", then "ok next" after Phase 1.

What changed

  • Ring view (fitRingHtml()), modelled on EVE Workbench's radial view and the EVE client's fitting window: the ship's render from CCP's image server in the middle, high slots on the top arc, mid slots on the right, low slots at the bottom, rigs then subsystems on the left. One tile per fitted module copy, showing the module's icon (its initials if the image doesn't load).
  • A tile is the same state button as a list row (button.ms-state with the same data-type-id / data-copy / data-from / data-to, built by moduleStateTileHtml() from the same moduleCopyStates() / nextModuleState()), so wireModuleStateControls() drives both views: a click moves that copy to the next allowed state, hover gives the name, state and any reason a state is skipped. Rigs and subsystems are fixed, as in the list.
  • State on a tile: cyan border and glow = active, grey = online, dashed and greyed icon = off, rigs and subsystems amber; plus the list's state dot in the corner (filled / hollow / dashed), so colour is never the only cue.
  • Charges and scripts in the racks are listed under the ring ("Loaded Gleam M ×6"); drones, fighters, implants, boosters and cargo stay as lists below it (moduleListWithStatesHtml(f, idToName, {skipSlots: true})).
  • Ring / List switch at the top of the Fit card (both analyzers). The choice is remembered in this browser (localStorage key dscanFitView, kept in memory if storage is blocked). With no choice yet, screens 600 px and wider start on the ring and phones on the list, as Workbench advises.
  • Tile size (fitRingGeometry()): one angular step for the whole fit from its fullest arc (at most 70° per arc, 13° between tiles) and the largest tile that doesn't touch its neighbour. A full eight-slot rack gives ~6% tiles (29 px on a 480 px ring), frigate racks the 8.4% maximum. Everything is a percentage of the ring, so it scales with its column.

Found on the way

  • The first spacing put eight tiles 8.4% apart along each 80° arc with 7.6% tiles. That is enough along the arc, but upright square tiles near the diagonals overlap unless they are a full tile apart on the worse axis: the full-rack fit had 10 overlapping pairs. Fixed by sizing from chord × cos(half-span) and narrowing the arcs to 70°.

Lesson for future models

  • LESSONS_LEARNED #33 (extended): anything placed by formula is checked at its worst case (here a full-rack fit, every pair of tile rectangles tested), not just on the fit you happen to be looking at.
  • Reuse the existing control markup and wiring for a new view of the same data (here the tile is the list's state button), so both views can never disagree on what a click does.
