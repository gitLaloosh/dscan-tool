What changed on the page (Damage Application chart)

  • Zones. Their EWAR that reaches you is shaded from 0 out to its range, in the EWAR / Misc card's badge colour, with its letter on a badge above the plot. Where two reach, the shading is darker.
    • T: their warp scrambler, and their furthest web (modules and web drones). These are the same projected inputs pyfa's graph applies.
    • D: their furthest target painter.
    • C: their furthest running energy neutralizer, and their furthest running nosferatu. A neut switched off on the Fit card leaves the chart.
  • Pins. Ranges that aren't EWAR are numbered pins, in distance order: their lock range and their drone control range. They replace the old dashed lines with text labels, which were dropped whenever they collided.
  • Badges never overlap. Badges and pins sit 20 px apart; one that's moved has a short leader to its line.
  • Key. One row per zone and pin, under the chart: badge, a swatch of its shade, what it is, and how far. Below it, one line: "Shaded: inside their range", with the whole explanation on hover.
    • The rows come from the fit alone, so they stay the same on every axis and setting.
    • Charted across your speed or signature, the zones aren't drawn and the line reads "Marked when charting across distance".
  • Values on the axes (5c).
    • Their tackle distances are printed in the x axis's number row in the tackle colour, and your range in white. Where your range equals a tackle distance, there's one number, yours.
    • The DPS at your range is printed on the y axis in each line's colour, with a dashed guide to its dot. When both lines give the same number, there's one white number.
    • Round tick numbers that would touch one of these are left out, and close labels are nudged apart with a leader. The axis end, which carries the unit, is always printed.
  • Nothing moves (subtext rule 4).
    • The plot's frame is fixed. The left edge comes from the widest number this fit's y axis can show, not the one shown now, and the top leaves room for the badge row.
    • While a slider is dragged (on the card or in the fight strip), the y scale can only grow, so the lines don't jump under the pointer. On release, it fits again.
  • The card's ⓘ panel explains the markers, and so does METHODOLOGY.md ("The fight").

Presentation only: no PYFA CORE section changed (the manifest check passes). The EWAR modules now record whether they are running (running), for the C zone. The badge letters are one shared table (EWAR_BADGE_LETTER) for the EWAR card and the chart.

  • The old checks read the marker labels ("their drone range") from the chart. They now read them from the key.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #51: a collision rule passes trivially when nothing collides. The first "no axis numbers touch" check passed even with the tick dropping switched off, because at the default settings nothing sat near a tick. Put the colliding case into the test on purpose, then prove the check bites with a mutation.
