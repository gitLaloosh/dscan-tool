"Your Side" renamed "Standings"

UITests 2026-10-28 · v2026.10.14.94 → v2026.10.14.95

The owner: "we need to rename 'your side'. feel free to give suggestions but i think 'Affiliations' or something along that line works". I suggested Standings, EVE's own word for who's blue and who's red, since "Affiliations" would sit too close to the Pilot Analyzer's Pilot Affiliation box. The owner: "Standings works. let's do it. no need to mock it up for me, let's just fix it."

What changed

  • The chip beside My Ship, its panel and its info text, the System Analyzer's note ("Set your Standings to mark friendlies"), the pilot tag's hover, and ERRORS.md all say Standings. The name lives in one place (CARD_NAMES.side), so that one change reached everything.
  • Two info-panel sentences read better with it: names are sorted "by your Standings"; the Pilot Affiliation box shows "where they stand in your Standings".
  • The descriptive "your side" (your alliance's losses and kills, as in "killed your side lately") stays: it describes your group, not the card.
  • Stored settings, element IDs and code names (side.*, dscanSide) are unchanged, so nothing you've saved is lost.
  • tools/check_card_names.py has a new RETIRED_EXACT list, matched with capitals, so "Your Side" can't come back while lower-case "your side" stays ordinary prose. A test copy with "Set Your Side to mark friendlies" is flagged.

Checks

  • system_analyzer_check.py expects "Standings" on the chip and in the note; v.94 fails both. affiliation_check.py and sa_mocks.py wording updated.
  • Regression suite ALL PASS (269 fits); the update-check chain; all 41 page checks; lints 0 problems.

Lesson for future models

When a retired name's words are also ordinary prose ("your side's losses"), retire it by its exact spelling, not lower-cased, or the lint pushes you to rewrite sentences that were never about the card. Here the card's name lived in one place (CARD_NAMES, LESSON #42's rule), which is why a rename touched one line of the page.
