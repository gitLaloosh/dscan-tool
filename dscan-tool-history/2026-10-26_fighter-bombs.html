What you see

  • The Bomb button is a toggle: a heavy bomber's Bomb (Ametat, Antaeus, Gungnir, Termite) can be clicked. It is off by default, as in pyfa, and its hover gives the numbers either way, e.g. "Micro Electron Bomb: 3,840 damage per squadron every 60 s (64 DPS)".
  • Switched on, the bomb is counted in:
    • the squadron's Firepower row;
    • Total DPS and volley;
    • Damage Application and the Matchup times.
  • How it lands: anywhere, with no lock needed, at min(1, your signature / its 400 m explosion).
  • Example: a Hel with two Ametat II squadrons goes from 1,471 to 1,599 DPS. Against the default target, Damage Application goes from 91 to 219.

Lesson for future models

Two lessons were applied rather than found again.

  • LESSON #65: the fighter DPS and application went to every check that reads them (check_application.py, check_matchup.py). Both had skipped bombs by name, and both were taught pyfa's bomb path.
  • LESSON #62: a formula that reads an attribute must have every pyfa handler on that attribute recorded, or asserted absent. The bomb reads attributes no table records, so the generator now asserts that nothing modifies them.
