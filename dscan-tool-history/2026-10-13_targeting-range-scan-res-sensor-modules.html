Targeting range, scan resolution and sensor strength modules

Feature backlogAudit batch 3 2026-10-13 · v2026.10.13.12 → v2026.10.13.13 · Erik: "start on the feature backlog starting with highest severity"

What was missing

Targeting range and scan resolution were calculated from the hull and skills only. No fitted module, rig or subsystem changed them. Sensor strength did include Sensor Boosters and Signal Amplifiers, but ignored scripts.

I parsed every pyfa eos/effects.py handler that changes the ship's maxTargetRange, scanResolution or scan*Strength, plus the handlers that change those modules' own bonuses (scripts and the interceptor role bonus). Values come from CCP's SDE.

Stacking follows pyfa's modifiedAttributeDict:

  • Each penalty group is penalized on its own.
  • Bonuses and penalties form separate chains, largest first.
  • Each step is 1 + (m − 1) × exp(−i²/7.1289).

Scripts are paired per instance by mid-slot flag, so two copies of the same booster with different scripts are handled correctly.

Left out on purpose

  • Integrated and Networked Sensor Arrays, plus the Bastion and Siege sensor effects, are "while active" capital modules. They belong to the module-toggle item.
  • Remote boosts and dampening from other ships are projected effects, out of scope.
  • Implants such as Long Range Targeting and Signature Analysis hardwirings are covered by the implants backlog item.
