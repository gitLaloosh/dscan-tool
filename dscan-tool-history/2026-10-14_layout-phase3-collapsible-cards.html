Found on the way

  • The summaries were first cut with an ellipsis when too long; the long-names check flagged the phone's Navigation summary ("… · 75 km") running past its card, hidden by the ellipsis. Summaries now wrap onto a second line, right-aligned, instead of losing numbers.

Lesson for future models

  • LESSONS_LEARNED #33 (extended): an ellipsis hides information; the layout check counts clipped text as overflow.
  • A collapsed summary must come from the same values the open card shows (computed in the same function), so the two can never disagree; the check compares them.
