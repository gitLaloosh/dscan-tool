Pilots, then ISK

Fleet tab 2026-11-01 · v2026.11.01.7

Why

With the kills grouped into fleets (v2026.11.01.6), each fleet heading shows its ISK on the right, but the kills under it had their ISK one column further left, with the fleet size where the heading's ISK sits. The owner, from a screenshot of the open list: "we should swap the fleet size and isk value placement."

What changed

  • Each kill in the Fleet tab's kill list now shows its pilots ("6 pilots", "solo"), then its ISK: the ISK sits under its fleet heading's ISK (a few pixels apart, depending on the heading's age text).
  • At 390 px the second line of a kill reads the same way: the pilots, then the ISK, last.

Checks

  • kill_groups_check asks for each kill's pilots before its ISK, wide and at 390 px. It fails on v2026.11.01.6; 2 mutants caught (the markup, the phone grid).
  • Its made-up kill ages moved to whole minutes: a kill 30 seconds old could tick between the page drawing "30 s ago" and the check reading it (LESSON #123).
  • Run: --auto chose intel, pilot, system: 12 lints + 36 page checks, 0 failed, 0 flaky.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #123: a check that compares a time-ago text must give it a value that can't tick between the page drawing it and the check reading it. A failure in an unrelated part of a check is a hint of a flake.
