What you see

  • No charge is the first row of each weapon's section in the Charges list. It works like a charge:
    • click it (or press Enter) to unload every copy;
    • drag it onto one weapon to unload that one;
    • drop it in the middle of the ring to unload every weapon.
    An unloaded weapon goes Online and its tile shows the weapon's own icon. A click on it switches Online / Offline only.
  • Loading a charge into an empty weapon makes it Active again. Nothing remembers what a weapon held before.
  • Use original charges, under the ring, puts back the fit's own charges. A weapon that had been unloaded goes Active.
  • Firepower names empty weapons, e.g. "5 weapons confirmed · 1 launcher empty", with the full sentence on hover. The Damage Application note and the Matchup's "why" say it too.
  • The hover bar. Hovering a weapon shows Unload charge · Charges… after a short pause; an empty weapon shows Charges… only.
    • Charges… opens the list at that weapon.
    • Keyboard focus shows the bar too.
    • On a phone, hold a finger on the weapon for the same choices. A tap still sets the state.
  • A ⋯ button at the start of the High, Mid and Low racks. Its menu has All active / All online / All offline; the High rack's adds Unload all charges and Charges….
    • All active is literal: every module is asked to be Active, and the state engine applies what pyfa allows (group limits, activation blocks), saying why for the rest.
    • A module with no active function goes Online, and an unloaded weapon stays Online.
  • The Charges list stays inside the Fit card at every width. Below 600 px it was a sheet over the page, and it covered the Matchup in the owner's narrow window.
    • It opens under the card's header and brings the card into view.
    • Names wrap on a phone.
    • Its how-to text is behind its own (i), "How the Charges list works".
    • A fit with more than one weapon type gets weapon chips beside the damage-type chips.

How it's matched to pyfa

  • Unloading changes inputs only. It changes the charge in that copy and its state; the engine then computes the fit as pyfa does. An Online weapon deals no damage and uses no capacitor (pyfa Module.capUse counts capacitorNeed only while Active).
  • Why Online: EVE can't run a weapon with no charge, while pyfa would let one be Active and drain capacitor for nothing. That choice is in DEVIATIONS.md.
  • Killmail weapons: a weapon a killmail shows empty keeps v.67's assumed charge, unless the user unloads it.

Bugs found on the way (fixed)

  • Capacitor drain with mixed copies. A charge's capacitor bonus (Void L +25%) was averaged over every copy of a weapon type, including copies that weren't Active. A Megathron with two of seven blasters unloaded drained 31.8 GJ/s; pyfa says 32.8. Now only the Active copies count, picked the same way as for damage. The capacitor simulation also gives copies with different charges their own capacitor need, as pyfa simulates each module.
  • The pyfa stand-in skipped empty slots (golden_oracle.py). It paired charges with copies by skipping empty slots, so the next copy got the charge, and pyfa was run on a different fit from the tool's. v.76's Drake golden only passed because its empty slot was the last one.
  • A weapon type with no Active copy could still get an assumed charge when another weapon type on the fit was cold. Now it doesn't, since it deals no damage.
  • The open list's DPS now follow state changes made while it is open (v.76 kept the old numbers until it was reopened).

Lesson for future models

LESSON #69: with per-copy inputs, keep each copy's place and each copy's state on both sides.

  • When copies of one module can differ, line the tool and pyfa up copy by copy: an empty slot keeps its place.
  • Every per-type sum or average must count only the copies in the state that matters, chosen as the damage loop chooses them.
  • Put the odd copy first in a constructed golden. Last is where a skipped one hides.
