What changed

  • One header line in the Pilot Analyzer:
    • the pilot's name;
    • a hull dropdown showing the hull on screen and its share of their losses ("Loki 2 of 5 losses ▾"), with their most-lost hulls inside;
    • the last kill as one line ("Last kill Vedmak · 9/1/2026 · Curse ›"), which opens to the full details, the zKillboard link, the "see their losses in that hull" button and the other attackers.
    These replace three rows: the last-kill box, the hull chips, and the "pilot — hull — 3 most recent losses" line. When the hull was typed or picked from the last kill, it is named plainly instead of in a dropdown.
  • An assessment box: the loss tabs sit on a box that holds everything about that loss. The Fit Analyzer result is the same box without tabs, with the fit's name and "Set as my ship" on top.
  • The scoreboard is now the threat / my ship switch, inside each loss and inside the Fit Analyzer box, above the cards:
    • Threat half: their DPS on you, and how long they last.
    • My-ship half: how long you last, and your DPS on them.
    • Middle: who has the edge (who outlasts the other; "neither breaks" when both hold) and who is faster.
    Click a half to show that ship below. The side you're on stays when you change loss tabs, and only the visible loss draws your ship. Changed numbers still turn amber (M6). On a phone the two halves sit side by side, with the edge and speed underneath, and the board stays at the top while you scroll.
  • Bug fixed (v.42, from Erik's screenshot): a probe launcher got a Weaknesses tile ("No ammo aboard"). The picks now skip the same weapon-like groups the engine skips (probe, bomb and interdiction sphere launchers, via weaponCategoryFromGroup).
  • Unchanged: no calculation or PYFA CORE section changed. The scoreboard shows the same numbers the strip did, plus the edge, which is just which of the two times is longer.

Lesson for future models

When you add mockup boards to an existing canvas, it still opens on its first page. Erik saw the old boards and asked whether he had the wrong file. Set the canvas's launch to the new page, or name the page in your message. Before describing what a mockup shows, render the board files yourself and send the images too, so the user has the options even if the canvas view goes wrong (LESSONS_LEARNED #41).
