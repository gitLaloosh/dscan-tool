The Threat Vector logo and the site's icons

Page 2026-11-07 · v2026.11.07.4

Why

  • Build queue 022, Project doc roadmap/logo-header-plan.md, mockup Claude outputs/logo_header_mockup.html (approved 2026-11-06: "looks good, let's get it in the queue"). The owner chose logo R37 ("R37 is the one"), left of the title, with the tops of the two T's lined up.
  • Built right after 023 (the owner: "would it just be better to do 23 first and then sort out how 22 affects it?"). The page had no tab icon, so browsers asked for /favicon.ico; since 023's policy that showed as a blocked image in the console.

What changed

  • Header: the logo, inline SVG, left of "Threat Vector": 1.6× the title's size (42 px; 39 px at 390, 32 px at 320), its T's top on the title's T's top (measured: 0 to 0.25 px off). Decoration only: aria-hidden, not focusable; the h1 still carries the name. Colours come from the page's tokens through CSS classes (SVG colour attributes can't take var(...)). No tile behind it in the header (the owner: "No tile"); the mockup had shown a faint one. The header's height doesn't change at any width.
  • Tab icon: favicon.svg (the monogram without orbits, which turn into an X at 16 px) and favicon-32.png. Phone home screen: apple-touch-icon.png, 180 px, the full logo on its tile's colour to the corners (iOS rounds them). The PNGs are rendered from the SVGs by tools/render_icons.py with the pre-installed Playwright; the logo's master is img/logo/threat-vector-logo.svg.
  • The policy: img-src gains 'self' so the site's own icons load (Firefox applies img-src to the tab icon). With an icon link, browsers no longer ask for /favicon.ico.
  • Nothing kept in the browser changes.

Checks

  • New logo_check.py: the logo inline before the h1, aria-hidden, not focusable; its colours the tokens, no tile; the T tops within 1 px at 1440, 390 and 320 px (the mockup's pxcheck.py method: inked rows of a 4× screenshot); the title row fits on one line with no sideways scroll; the icon links point at files that exist, the PNGs 32 and 180 px. csp_check.py: the three icons load under the policy. render_icons.py --check is a quick lint. check_pii.py allows the two icon PNGs by name (image files are otherwise refused outside the game-icon folders).
  • Mutants, each caught: the T offset removed (all three widths), aria-hidden dropped, the tile put back, an orbit in another colour, the touch icon missing, 'self' left out of the policy (csp_hash --check, and csp_check: the icons blocked).
