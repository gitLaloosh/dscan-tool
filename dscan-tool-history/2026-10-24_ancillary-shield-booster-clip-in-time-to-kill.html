Fix: an Ancillary Shield Booster's clip in "you last" / "they last"

Fix 2026-10-24 · v2026.10.14.56 → v2026.10.14.57.

The owner (2026-10-24, Pilot Analyzer screenshot, his Osprey Navy Issue against an Orthrus): "seeing discrepancies in the various calculated stats ... would the EFT fits for both ships help you investigate?" He sent both fits. After the findings: "lets go with your recommendation", which was to simulate the clip.

What was checked

Both fits were run through the tool's engine and through pyfa's own code (projected effects and damage application, by item name). At 48.9 km, with the Orthrus stopped and the Osprey orbiting at 2,500 m/s, every damage number matched exactly:

  • Orthrus on you: 470.44, all thermal (Inferno Fury);
  • you on the Orthrus: 87.93, all EM (Acolyte II ×5; your rapid light missiles don't reach);
  • top speeds 2,500 and 2,390 m/s;
  • hit points and resists on both ships.

The screen's numbers agreed with each other. They looked different because the threat's Damage Application chart shows their damage on you, while Weaknesses ("your guns") shows yours on them.

The bug

  • What the time to kill did: it stopped every local repairer when the capacitor simulation ran dry, 120 s for the Osprey and 100 s for the Orthrus.
  • Why that's wrong for an ASB: an Ancillary Shield Booster on charges uses no capacitor. pyfa's calculateSustainableTank treats it the same way.
  • What the rule missed: the ASB stops for a different reason, to reload. An X-Large holds 112 m³, nine Navy Cap Booster 400s (12 m³ each). That gives 45 s of repairs, then a 60 s reload.
  • Why no check caught it: check_matchup.py left every ASB fit out ("an ancillary booster's rate isn't a plain amount / duration"), so this case had never been compared.

Fix

  • The engine lists each local repairer (exact.localReps). An ASB with charges aboard gets its clip:
    • clip = pyfa numShots = floor(capacity ÷ charge volume) ÷ chargeRate;
    • reload = reloadTime, 60 s;
    • charges = a full loaded clip plus the same charge in the cargo hold or fleet hangar. The whole hold counts for the ASB, even when a Capacitor Booster draws the same charges.
  • ttkSeconds now steps through the fight, segment by segment (ttkLayerEnd):
    • the ASB reps through its clip, reloads, and repeats while charges last, then stops;
    • every other repairer still runs until the capacitor runs dry;
    • a layer that's full stays full.
  • Unchanged cases:
    • An ASB with no charge at all (a pasted fit with none loaded or in the hold) runs on the capacitor, as before.
    • On a killmail with no charge seen, the tool assumes the booster was charged. It also runs until the capacitor runs dry, as before.
    • Ancillary Armor Repairers (paste boost and reload) are not followed yet. That gap is listed in DEVIATIONS.md.
  • The alternative not taken is written in METHODOLOGY.md so it can be brought back: pyfa's "factor in reload" average over the whole fight. For an X-Large ASB with Navy Cap Booster 400s that's 84 HP/s instead of 196. It hides the reload gap, which is where a ship running an ASB usually breaks.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #53: an independent check's exclusions are where bugs hide. check_matchup.py passed for weeks with ASB fits filtered out, and the first real ASB pair showed the rule was wrong. When a check skips a case "because it's not simple", write down what it skips and close it next, or test that case some other way before relying on the number.
