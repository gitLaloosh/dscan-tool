What changed

  • New PYFA CORE section ewar-calc (17 sections locked): EWAR_EFFECTS (149 effects, 196 rows) and EWAR_SKILL_ROWS (22), generated by the new tools/gen_ewar_table.py from every pyfa handler that modifies one of the 21 module attributes the card shows, from every carrier: hulls, modules, rigs, subsystems, skills (at V) and loaded scripts (charge effects on their own module, target 'self'). Filters are recorded as (skills AND groups) and checked exactly against every published module. pyfaEwarSetup() pools the rows with the same pyfaModPool / pyfaModValue as speed and resistances (stacking groups exact), plus the ship mode.
  • Engine: every fit whose hull has effects gets its EWAR entries from ewar-calc, one per (module type, loaded script); scripts are paired with their module by mid-slot flag. The four hand tables stay only as the fallback for a hull record without effects.
  • Card: copies of a module with different scripts are listed separately with the script's name; the type count counts names.
  • Recorder fix (tools/pyfa_effects.py): ProbeItem.getAttribute returns the item's real base attribute. Before, a filter like Effect2982's getAttribute('duration') was always false and looked like "no hits" (LESSON #28). Every other generator is unchanged by it (all still "up to date").
  • Skipped and reported by the generator: overheat, rows that only reach non-EWAR modules, charges no EWAR module can load (structure and HIC scripts), the Alliance Tournament cruiser's security-status web bonus (DEVIATIONS.md), command bursts / titan effects (projected, not modelled).

Lesson for future models

  • LESSONS_LEARNED #9 (extended): a "pyfa-verified" pin verifies only the exact fit pyfa was given. The Curse's -29.5% came from a script-less pyfa fit; the golden fit's killmail has scripts.
  • #26 (extended): scripts are carriers too; they modify their own module.
  • #28 (extended): a filter can fail silently when the probe lacks a method the filter calls (getAttribute).
