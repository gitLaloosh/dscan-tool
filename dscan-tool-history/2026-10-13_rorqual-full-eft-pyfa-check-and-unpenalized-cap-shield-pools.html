How the set bonus works (from CCP's SDE): each CA implant has a base bonus of 1.5% and an implantSetChristmas multiplier: 1.5, 1.4, 1.3 and 1.2. With all four plugged in, every implant's bonus is multiplied by the product of the four, which is 3.276. So 1.5% × 3.276 = 4.914%.

This closes the "Rorqual align ~5% off" item left open in v.9. It was the implant, not a missing module.

Bug found and fixed: cap/shield modules were stacking-penalized

The capacitor-capacity check turned up a real bug. The 2026-10-12 work stacking-penalized the cap and shield capacity and recharge multipliers. pyfa's handlers for these modules don't pass a stackingPenalties flag, so in pyfa (and in EVE) they stack at full strength:

  • capacitorCapacityMultiply
  • shieldCapacityMultiply
  • modifyPowerRechargeRate
  • modifyShieldRechargeRate
  • shieldOperationRechargeratebonusPostPercentOnline

The affected modules are Power Diagnostic System, Capacitor Power Relay, Capacitor Flux Coil, Capacitor Recharger, Shield Flux Coil, Shield Power Relay and Shield Recharger.

The fix adds two small helpers, unpenalizedReduction() and unpenalizedBonus(). They replace the stacking calls in four places: shield HP, shield recharge time, capacitor capacity and capacitor recharge time.

  • Rorqual: 3× Caldari Navy PDS gives capacity 84,375 × 1.05³ = 97,675 GJ (it was 95,082). EHP is now 1,735,654 (it was 1,714,471).
  • Ishtar (a pilot): capacity is now 2,576 GJ (it was 2,660). pyfa's own figure for that fit is 2,580, so the long-standing "~3% off" capacity gap is closed.
  • Rokh (3× PDS II): shield HP 161,706 → 166,114.

Display bug fixed: Weaknesses ring time

On this fit, the Weaknesses-card ring showed 38s while its own caption said "even at best case assuming its Capacitor Booster has charges". The Capacitor card said 37m10s for the same thing. The ring was reading the no-booster time instead of the best-case time its caption describes.

The fix adds capacitor.weakTimeToEmptySec / weakTimeToEmptyCapped and makes the ring use them. The ring now reads 37m10s. This was confirmed in a Playwright render.

Still open: capacitor drain

pyfa's −5.98 GJ/s can't be reproduced yet, for two reasons:

  • It depends on which modules were switched on in pyfa: bursts, smartbomb, both boosters, hardeners.
  • The cap-need skills and rigs are still unmodeled. On this fit that means the Capital Core Defense Capacitor Safeguard rig and Capital Shield Operation. These are in the next audit batch, "cap drain".

Pulse Activated Nexus Invulnerability Core has no dogma effect in pyfa's data and is correctly ignored for stats. It could be a candidate for a Misc flag.
