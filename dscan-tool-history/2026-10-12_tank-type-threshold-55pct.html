Tank Type threshold lowered 60% → 55%

2026-10-12 · dscan-tool.html · per Erik's request

Change

TANK_TYPE_THRESHOLD (a layer's share of raw, pre-resist HP needed to be called the fit's "tank type" rather than "Mixed") changed from 0.6 to 0.55. Pure constant change -- no other logic touched.

Verification

This constant had never been flattened/asserted in the regression suite before (tank type was display-only, never pinned). Added a flattening block to run_regression.mjs (tankTypeKind, shieldSharePct, armorSharePct, hullSharePct) and scanned all 74 existing golden fits under both the old and new threshold to find real, naturally-occurring cases rather than constructing synthetic ones:

  • vedmak_zkb138993362_precursor sits at 58% armor HP share -- "Mixed" under the old 60% threshold, now classified "armor". Pinned as the positive case.
  • vedmak_zkb139012125_precursor_role_bonus sits at 54% armor HP share -- just below the NEW 55% threshold, stays "Mixed" under both. Pinned as the boundary negative control.

Together these two real fits bracket the exact threshold (54% stays Mixed, 58% flips to armor), confirming the change takes effect exactly where intended with no off-by-one error.

Full suite: 74/74 fits, ALL PASS.
