What you get

  • Fleet / Groups: above "pilot rows up to" (15) hostile + neutral pilots, the System Analyzer opens on the fleet view; Groups shows the step 1 columns (every pilot still one click away).
  • The 5 largest groups (hostile first, then neutral) are looked up; the others are listed under "Not looked up" with a Look up button.
  • Each group: what it's been flying, from its 50 newest kills: its 3 main hulls with their share, ≈ how many of its pilots in local that is (an estimate: share × their count in local), and how many are on your D-Scan.
  • Common fit per hull (D5): from the group's 20 newest losses in it, grouped by exact fitted modules; up to 3 variants of 2+ losses with how many and what differs from the most common ("MWD instead of AB"); pick one and its numbers follow (DPS with damage types, EHP, weakest resist, EWAR letters). One fit only: "Fits vary, showing the latest"; no losses: "fit unknown". › opens the fit in the Fit Analyzer.
  • Their fleet, estimated: ≈ counts × each common fit, added up: raw DPS by damage type, what most of their ships are weakest to, how many have tackle / disruption / cap warfare / bursts, and logistics.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #75: on a killmail a weapon and the charge loaded in it are two items with the same flag. The first draft of the charge swap removed every high-slot item in a flag that held a charge, weapons included; it was caught in review. Pick items by type, never by the flag alone, keep the original item when it's already right, and give the check a loss that really needs the swap (here a Loki with Scourge in its launchers) so a mutant that drops the flag fails.
