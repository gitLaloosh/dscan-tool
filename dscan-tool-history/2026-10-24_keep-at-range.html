Fight setup F2: Keep at Range for both ships

Feature 2026-10-24 · v2026.10.14.52 → v2026.10.14.53. Plan: roadmap/turret-application-plan.md "Keep at Range in pyfa's inputs"; roadmap/subtext-plan.md section 5 (phase F2).

The owner (2026-10-24): add "Keep at range" next to Orbiting: "if one ship is faster than the other, and is outside of the target's scram/web range, it's possible for them to maintain a specified distance from the target." Then: in-game names for both ships (Stopped / Approach / Orbit / Keep at Range).

What changed on the page

  • Keep at Range for you and for the threat, in the fight strip's menus and on the Damage Application card's Adjust panel.
  • How it moves. The ship keeping range moves straight away from the other ship at the other's closing speed, up to its own top speed, so the distance holds. The closing speed is the other ship's speed when it approaches, and 0 when it sits still, orbits or keeps range too. Its speed slider is greyed out, because the speed follows the other ship; the readout says "holding, 1,613 m/s".
  • Only where it holds. The choice is not offered inside the other ship's scram or web range, or when the other ship closes faster than it can fly. The menu's line gives the reason, for example "Can't: inside their scram (9 km) and web (10 km)" or "Can't: you're faster (1,691 m/s)".
  • Chosen, then no longer possible (say you move the range inside their web): it stays chosen, so nothing changes behind your back. It is marked amber on the strip and the card, the reason is on hover, the readout says "can't hold", and the speed is capped at the ship's top.
  • The card's info panel and the Matchup's explain it. METHODOLOGY.md has a new section on the fight, and DEVIATIONS.md lists the mapping.

The example from the plan, now on the page: the Hecate approaching at full speed (1,613 m/s), your Vexor (top 1,691 m/s) keeping range at 15 km, outside its 9 km scram and 10 km web. The result is 0 DPS applied to you ("holds"), 291 DPS applied to them, and they last 26 s.

How it maps onto pyfa

pyfa's damage graph has no "keep at range". It takes each ship's speed and angle to the line of sight, and the transversal is |atkSpeed·sin(atkAngle) − tgtSpeed·sin(tgtAngle)| (_calcAngularSpeed). Keep at Range passes angle 180° (moving straight away, no transversal) and the holding speed. Missiles and drones read that speed directly. These are inputs only (PYFA_CORE rule); no core section changed. The chart stays a snapshot at one distance, as pyfa's graph: it doesn't play the fight out over time.

Lesson for future models

When a movement or setting can't exist in some situations, don't silently switch the user's choice when the situation changes. Offer it only where it's possible. If it becomes impossible later, keep it, mark it, and say why. A check then has three cases to cover: possible, not offered, and chosen-but-impossible (fight_strip_check.py does all three).
