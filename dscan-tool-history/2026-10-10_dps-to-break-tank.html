DPS required to break an active-rep tank

Date: 2026-10-10   Status: Delivered, verified via 28/28 regression PASS + an 11/11 direct unit test against hand-derived numbers + a real-Chromium render check

Requested by Erik: the natural follow-up to the Active Rep/Buffer Tank indicator — "how much DPS would be required to break their tanking threshold." Scoped over two short discussions: (1) a calculation-feasibility pass (verified live ESI attributes for repair amount/cycle time, and a real, non-obvious asymmetry: armor/hull reps get a verified level-V "Repair Systems" skill speed-up, Shield Boosters don't); (2) Ancillary Armor Repairers needed their own scoping call — Erik chose to show both the burst (charge-boosted) and sustained (unboosted) numbers, labeled, rather than picking just one.

The change

Each layer's active-rep badge (inside the existing Tank Type highlight box) now shows the DPS an attacker needs to sustain to out-repair it, instead of "coming in a later update." Shield: shieldBonus / duration, no skill multiplier (confirmed — Shield Operation only speeds up passive regen, not the booster itself). Armor/Hull: repairAmount / (duration × 0.75), the 0.75 being max-level (V) "Repair Systems" (confirmed to cover both armor and hull reps, 5%/level cycle-time reduction). An Ancillary Armor Repairer additionally shows a separate burst figure using its own chargedArmorDamageMultiplier attribute (confirmed live — roughly 3x on a Large Ancillary Armor Repairer, matching its real "Boosted repair multiplier" stat) for while its loaded Nanite Repair Paste lasts; Ancillary Shield Boosters deliberately do NOT get this treatment, since their own live ESI dogma confirmed they have no equivalent attribute at all — their Cap Booster charges only change where the activation cost is drawn from, not the repair amount. A fit with more than one qualifying rep module (rare, but possible) sums them before computing the final HP/s. Zero new ESI calls — reuses the exact dogma already being fetched for the Active Rep/Buffer Tank badge.
