the owner asked: "are we not recommending fighters when my ship is not active?" They weren't: with no ship saved, the Weaknesses card had Drone, Missile and turret tiles, but nothing for a carrier pilot. His decisions:

  • "yes please", and "let's have those that do multiple rows be able to expand and collapse. default to collapsed";
  • from the mockup: collapsed "B: names + how much lands";
  • the bomber row: "Count the bomb".

What you see

  • A Fighter tile after the Missile tile, labelled "Fighter · Minmatar" (the race of the least resisted damage type). It names the T2 light fighter, heavy fighter and bomber of that type and how much of each one's damage lands on this ship.
  • Collapsed (the default): one line each.
    • Fighter: "Einherji · Tyrfing · Gungnir", then "light 79% · heavy 2% · bomber 44%".
    • Missile: "6 launchers", then "6 of 6 land 95% or more" (with the prop module off).
  • Open (the chevron by the label): the rows, as the Missile tile had them since v.69. For fighters: Light / Heavy / Bomber, the % with the target's MWD or afterburner off and on, under 50% amber. The hover gives the one decimal; the bomber's says it counts "its attack and its bomb".
  • Open or closed is kept per fit: it survives a re-render (closing and opening the card, a module toggle), and a new paste starts collapsed. Switching it doesn't redraw the card.
  • The card's info explains the fighter tile and the collapsing.

Heavy fighters land almost nothing on a subcap: their torpedoes are anti-capital (a subcap's fighter-missile resistance lets 10% through), and their attack missile's explosion is huge. On a capital they land fully.

How it's matched to pyfa

  • The types (FIGHTER_REC_TYPES): for each damage type, the T2 fighter of each squadron role (light attack, heavy attack, bomber) that deals only that type. Superiority and support fighters aren't offered, and neither are faction fighters.
  • The fighter: your skills at V and no carrier bonus (pyfaProjFighterSetup on an empty ship); abilities pyfa's defaults, plus the bomb on the bomber.
  • How much lands: pyfa's getFighterAbilityMult (PYFA CORE application, unchanged) for each ability, weighted by that ability's DPS:
    • at distance 0 (fighters fly to the target), lock range ignored, mobile mode auto;
    • at the signature and speed the missile ranking uses, with the prop module off and on;
    • against the target's own fighter-missile resistances.
  • No PYFA CORE change. The new code only builds inputs and draws the tile.

Golden fixtures: one shared file

The tile fetches the same 12 fighters and 4 bombs for every fit. Each golden fit answers fetches only from its own records, so every fit would have failed with a 404; copying 16 records into 263 fits would have added megabytes.

  • New regression_suite/shared_types.json (35 KB), written by refresh_golden_fixtures.py --write from the SDE and index.html's table.
  • Every harness that answers fetches from a fixture falls back to it: run_regression.mjs, dump_results.mjs, compare_cap_sim.mjs, scaffold_golden_fit.mjs. A fit's own record still wins.
  • New pins fighterRec:<class>:<name|cruising|boosted> on three fits: an afterburner cruiser, an MWD battleship, a capital. They equal pyfa's values (the check below). A break that ignores the target's resistances fails the capital's heavy pin (55 instead of 100).

Lesson for future models

LESSON #66: a fetch every fit makes, whatever it flies, needs a shared fixture that every harness serves.

  • Put those types in shared_types.json, written from the page's own table so it can't drift.
  • Find every harness by grepping for the escaped regex universe\/types. A plain grep for universe/types found only one of the five.
  • Pin the new output on a few fits, so a fixture that lost the shared records fails the suite.
