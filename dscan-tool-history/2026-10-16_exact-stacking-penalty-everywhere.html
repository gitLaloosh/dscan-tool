What was wrong

  • applyStacking / applyStackingBonus used a stacking-penalty table rounded to 3 decimals (1, 0.869, 0.571, 0.283, 0.106, 0.030, 0.006, 0.001, then 0). pyfa uses the exact exp(-i² / 7.1289) (0.86912, 0.57058, 0.28296, ...).
  • Still affected:
    • turret and missile damage and rate-of-fire modules and rigs;
    • shield boost amplifiers;
    • sensor strength;
    • the legacy speed and mass path, and EWAR rigs on fallback hulls.
    Drones, fighters, resists, speed and EWAR on current hulls already used the exact formula.

What changed

  • Both helpers are now one-line wrappers over the core's pyfaPenalizedProduct, which keeps separate chains for bonuses and reductions, strongest first. Mixed signs in one pool now split into those two chains, as in pyfa. The table STACKING_PENALTY is removed.
  • The "Rounded stacking table" entry is gone from DEVIATIONS.md, and PYFA_CORE.md is updated.
  • compare_fit_stats.py drops its 3e-4 tolerance on weapon DPS and sensor strength: every field now compares at 1e-6.

Lesson for future models

  • LESSONS_LEARNED #7 (extended): a tolerance that exists for one known gap hides every other gap of that size. Remove the gap, then the tolerance.
