  • One fight.
    • The range and movements set on this card are the same settings as the strip under the board and the threat's card. Each one follows the others.
    • A threat card left collapsed updates its row (LESSON #43).

Lesson for future models

LESSON #52: an input's value attribute is where it started, not where it is. A check read the threat card's slider with get_attribute('value'), got the value the card was drawn with (4400), and failed a correct page. Read the live property instead (el.value, input_value()).
