Firepower: hull bonuses generated from pyfa (hand-tables Phase 1)

Bug fixPYFA CORE 2026-10-14 · v2026.10.14.2 → v2026.10.14.3 · the owner: "ok start on the firepower fix"

What changed

  • Generated table, new core section hull-damage. SHIP_DAMAGE_MOD_EFFECTS holds 340 hull and subsystem effects as 458 rows, written by the new tools/gen_hull_damage_table.py from pyfa's handlers. Each row carries:
    • the target (weapon, the loaded charge, drone, or fighter Attack Missile)
    • damage or rate of fire
    • the source attribute and per-level scaling
    • pyfa's exact filter: skills and groups, checked on the weapon or on the charge
    • the damage type
    The generator verifies that every filter reproduces pyfa on all charge × weapon pairings. One row is stacking-penalized (Claw); it joins the Gyrostabilizer pool, as in pyfa.
  • Applied per weapon instance. Missiles get each damage type's own bonus, which replaces the "dominant damage type" approximation. Rate of fire is per weapon, including the capacitor-drain loop. Drones and fighters get their rows too.
  • SHIP_ROLE_BONUS (~340 hand-typed hulls) is now only the fallback for 7 special-edition hulls with no SDE damage effects. SUBSYSTEM_ROLE_BONUS is retired: subsystems are carriers in the generated table.
  • Modules: the Drone Damage Amplifier effect (6556) is now gated by effect, not group. That gives the bonus to the faction and Abyssal Heat Sinks, Magnetic Field Stabilizers and BCSs that carry it, and applies DDA bonuses to fighters as well as drones, as pyfa does.
  • Skills:
    • Vorton Power Amplification ×1.15 on all Vorton damage
    • XL Torpedo and XL Cruise Missile Specialization (T2 XL launchers)
    • Mutated Drone Specialization
  • Tooling: the update-check chain includes the new generator and the fallback audit; audit_firepower.py now checks the fallback list.

Not changed (now listed in DEVIATIONS.md)

  • Fighter abilities other than Attack Missile aren't modeled, including the Caiman / Komodo +100% fighter turret-attack role bonus. The generator lists them as skipped.
  • Alliance Tournament hulls whose bonus scales with pilot security status (Sidewinder).
  • The Skua is still missing from the Tactical Destroyer mode table (P2/P3, with the mode tables).

Lesson for future models

  • A charge bonus may test the weapon's skill rather than the charge's. A Legion subsystem filters on the launcher needing Missile Launcher Operation, so a test against one launcher matched plain projectile ammo. Generate filters against every charge × weapon pairing and record which side each test applies to.
  • When a diff moves a value by exactly 1 on an x.5 number, check for a rounding tie before hunting a bug.
