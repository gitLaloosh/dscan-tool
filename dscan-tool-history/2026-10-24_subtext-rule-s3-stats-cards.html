What changed on the page

  • Every card has an ⓘ. Firepower, Resistance, Navigation & Targeting and Capacitor each have an info button in the title row. How the card's numbers are worked out is in its panel.
  • Firepower.
    • One short line under each tile: "5 weapons confirmed", "none in bay", "all at once". The full sentence is on hover.
    • Each weapon row is the charge, with one line under it: the weapon and its range ("Light Neutron Blaster II · 2.8 + 1.6 km"). The DPS is on the right. The separate range column is gone; it used to wrap or move under the name in a narrow card. Drone rows read "range set by its pilot".
  • Resistance. The 456-character note under the Repairs strip (what Break Point means, the skills assumed, what a dash means) moved to the ⓘ panel.
  • Navigation & Targeting. One line per tile:
    • "225 m/s without MWD" and "8.7 s with MWD on";
    • "20 base + 40 skills" for drone control range;
    • "402 m with MWD on" for signature radius;
    • "Magnetometric" for sensor strength ("· module bonus" when a Sensor Booster or similar adds to it).
    A fit with both an afterburner and an MWD shows both on the one line ("AB 1,200 · off 225 m/s").
  • Capacitor. The caption under the ring is one line, for example "best case · ASB on charges" or "no booster to offset the drain". The net rate's line is "8.3 recharge − 8.6 drain". Hover either for the sentence it replaced.
  • Readable grey. Text in the too-faint grey (--text-faint, under 4.5:1 contrast on the cards) now uses the dim grey (--text-dim) everywhere. That covers the resist percentages, the dashes in the Repairs strip, the version tag and the module "Offline" label. Only three disabled controls keep the faint grey: the two reset icons at their defaults, and "Use in assessments" with no ship saved.
  • In a very narrow card, the Resistance total and its ⓘ go under the title instead of past the card's edge.

No calculation changed. Regression suite unchanged (ALL PASS).

Checks

  • check_subtext.py (update-check chain): text in --text-faint is now a failure. A disabled control may keep it, marked faint: disabled on its line. v.49 fails 24 times, v.50 passes. Now 10 info panels and 21 context lines in the code.
  • no_shift_check.py: every card is a hard fail at 1440, 1100 and 375 px, and so is anything else it measures. v.49 already passes too, because nothing moved after S2. This step guards against a future change that brings movement back; it doesn't prove a fix.
  • missing_charges_check.py: reads the Capacitor card's short caption and its hover sentence (LESSON #48).
  • layout_check.py now opens 8 info panels per fit (dmg, weak, ewar, fit, fp, rs, nav, cp), and all stay on screen and inside their column. card_width_check.py caught the Resistance header past a 300 px card edge, which is now fixed.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #48: moving text into a hover hides it from checks that read the page. inner_text doesn't include title text, so three caption checks failed although the page was right. Before shortening a line, search the checks for its words. Point each check at the new short line and the hover, so it still proves the same fact; don't delete or loosen it. Also: when you turn a report into a hard failure, run it on the old build. If the old build passes too (as no_shift_check did here), say that it's a guard, not proof of the change.
