What changed

  • Near You's height (over 900 px): the list is as tall as the map column and scrolls inside it; it is never shorter than the map's smallest box and its heading (IT_NEAR_FLOOR, 430 px), so the Feed starts right under the two. 900 px and under is unchanged. "+N more systems below" says how many rows are out of view, written after a draw and on the list's scroll (never on a timer). Opening a row low in the list scrolls the list to show it, and pressing a system on the map that keeps its route scrolls the list to its row; hovering and un-pinning never scroll, and nothing scrolls the page. A redraw keeps the list's scroll and the focus.
  • Feed filter chips (every width): pressing a system, a corporation or alliance on a pilot chip, or a ship adds a chip on its own line under the filter bar ("Only" · chips · "Clear all" with two or more). The same press again, or the chip's ×, removes it. A second system replaces the first. Every chip and the typed search must match. The search box stays the user's (a group press used to write its ticker there). Near You is never filtered; the map reads the system chip. Chips aren't kept after a reload.
  • Ship names as buttons: the ship chips in the Feed and the ship names in Near You's rows. A kill matches a ship that died or was among the attackers.
  • ISK on Near You's kill badges: each badge adds up its kills' values in amber ("2 friendlies lost 194.0M"); the hover has the exact total and how many of the kills had a value. From the kills already read; nothing new is asked.
  • Nothing new is kept in the browser.
