Why it looked that way

The Pilot Analyzer's Fit card was still the v.22 module-states <details> with a "hide" / "show modules and states" text link (from when it was the only collapsible card), and the Fit Analyzer's Fit card couldn't collapse at all. Collapsible cards (v.18) used a different mechanism and never covered the Fit card.

What changed

  • One toggle: the Fit card's title is the same toggle as every other card (chevron, cardIsOpen(f, 'fit'), focus kept). The old <details>, its "hide" link and f.fitCardOpen are gone. Default: open in the Fit Analyzer; in the Pilot Analyzer open from 900 px up and closed on a phone, as before.
  • Header: the "Module states assumed: best case" label is removed (the assumption is explained in "How effective HP and DPS are calculated"). The header holds the title, a small reset (reload) icon button and the Ring / List switch. The reset button is always there, disabled while every module and drone is in its default state and cyan once something was changed. It replaces the "Custom states" label and the "Reset to best case" text button, with the same wiring.
  • Collapsed: the row shows the fit's name in the stat-label style used for "TOTAL DPS" (small capitals): the ship and the EFT fit name in the Fit Analyzer (e.g. "HECATE — MODE TEST"), the ship and the loss date in the Pilot Analyzer.
  • Presentation only.

Lesson for future models

  • When a new UI pattern replaces an older one (here collapsible cards vs the Fit card's <details>), migrate every place the old one is used in the same change, or the page ends up with two ways to do the same thing; Erik spotted the mismatch from one screenshot.
