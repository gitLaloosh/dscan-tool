The matchup strip: who wins, at a glance

Feature 2026-10-23 · v2026.10.14.40 → v2026.10.14.41 · This is My Ship phase 2a. The owner's decisions: split phase 2 (strip, tags and amber first); "you last" by damage type with reps; drones ported first (done in v.40); the strip follows the damage card's settings, mirrored. Weaknesses flipping to "your guns / their guns" is phase 2b.

What changed

  • A strip beside the threat / my ship switch whenever a ship is saved and "Use in assessments" is on. It looks the same in both views, in the Fit Analyzer and on every Pilot losses tab, where it follows the visible tab.
    CellWhat it is
    Their DPS on youthe "Their damage on you" card's headline
    Your DPS on themthe same fight mirrored: your weapons and drones on them, your webs, painters and scram applied, your speed as the attacker's and theirs as the target's
    You last / They lasttime to kill: their damage by type against your resists, shield then armor then hull, minus your local reps and shield regen while your capacitor lasts. "holds" when the damage never gets through
    Fasterwhose top speed is higher in the current states, and by how much
    The line under the strip says which fight it describes. Moving the damage card's sliders moves the strip.
  • Changed numbers in amber (M6). When you change your ship's module states, every strip cell and every number on your ship's cards that differs from your pasted fit turns amber. Hovering shows the pasted fit's value. The Fit card's reset clears it.
  • Phone: four cells in two rows ("Faster" hidden, as in the mockup), sticky at the top with the switch.
  • Engine inputs: each weapon and drone now carries its DPS by damage type (typeDps), outside the pyfa core. The damage card's "their speed" now tops out at their speed in their current states (their prop module counts only if it is running).
  • Recorded in DEVIATIONS.md: time to kill is not a pyfa number. It uses the shield's peak passive regen (as the Break Point does). It doesn't count their neuts on your capacitor or remote reps, and the card's MWD switch changes your speed and signature, not your capacitor.

Verification

  • New tools/independent_checks/check_matchup.py: 10 threats × 8 of my ships × 192 card settings, 14,976 points, 0 mismatches.
    • It covers 16,108 finite times, 4,113 of them after the capacitor ran out, and 13,844 "holds".
    • Every number is built independently: the DPS by damage type from pyfa's projected.py + application.py run unmodified on oracle fits, with each module's own damage types; both top speeds; and the time-to-kill rule written again from its description, on pyfa's HP, resonances and repair rates.
    • The check catches broken logic. Mutations give:
      • no capacitor cut-off: 34 mismatches;
      • no passive regen: 73;
      • mirror swapped: 25;
      • damage types ignored: 74.
    • In the update-check chain (now 33 steps).
  • New tools/page_checks/matchup_strip_check.py: 18 checks, 0 failures, at 1440 and 375 px. The cells come from the page's own function, and "Their DPS on you" equals the card headline. The range slider moves the strip. Switching the armor repairer off turns "You last" amber (21 s → 19 s) along with the changed card numbers, and reset clears them. With "Use in assessments" off the strip is gone. Loss tabs are followed, and the phone layout is checked.
  • Everything else: all other page checks pass (My Ship, layout, damage card, card widths, busy indicator, missing charges, zKillboard outage). The regression suite passes and the PYFA CORE lock is unchanged.

Lesson for future models

When you invent a number (here time to kill), check it two ways. Check the inputs against pyfa. Then write the rule a second time from its plain-language description and compare the two versions. The mutations show what each kind of mistake looks like, so a failure points at its cause. Also choose test fits that reach every branch: armor and shield reps whose capacitor runs out at different times, stable reps, and drone ships. A pool of fits that mostly "holds" tests very little.
