What was missing

The Repairs strip and Break Point used each repairer's base amount. Its cycle time was already right (v.11). No hull bonus changed the amount, nor did any rig. Shield Boost Amplifiers were applied through a group gate. Bastion, Siege and Triage local-rep effects weren't modeled at all.

I parsed every pyfa eos/effects.py handler that changes a fitted module's shieldBonus, armorDamageAmount or structureDamageAmount, or the ship's shieldRechargeRate. Values come from CCP's SDE. The rows were added to the existing MODULE_ATTR_MOD_EFFECTS table, with a new flag for stacking-penalized entries.

Bastion leaves cap drain unchanged on purpose: cap need and cycle time both drop ×0.8, so GJ/s is the same. The cap loop now uses the same effect-derived cycle multiplier as the HP/s figure.
