Drones in space: bandwidth squares in the Fit card

Feature 2026-10-13 · v2026.10.13.22 → v2026.10.13.23 · Erik: "only the bay but rather than a number that they toggle, perhaps give a visual representation in each drone's row that represents the limit based on the fit's drone bandwidth that the user can activate and deactivate... like pyfa"

What changed

  • Drone bay header. The drone bay in the Fit card shows the budget ("Bandwidth 60 / 75 Mbit/s · 5 / 5 in space") and a bandwidth bar.
  • One square per drone. Each drone type has a square for every drone of that type in the bay; filled means in space. The row also shows count in space, bandwidth each and DPS each.
  • Click to change. Clicking square k puts k of that type in space. Clicking the last filled square takes one back.
  • Limits, as in pyfa. Squares the free bandwidth or the 5-drone limit won't allow are dashed and do nothing. Hovering says why, e.g. "needs 10 Mbit/s each, 0 of 75 Mbit/s free — take other drones back first". Other drones have to come back before new ones can go out.
  • Non-combat drones count. EWAR and logistics drones use bandwidth and a drone slot, with no DPS.
  • Default unchanged. With no drone selection, the engine still assumes the best case: hardest-hitting combat drones first. The full output diff of every existing golden fit was empty.
  • Reset to best case clears drones and module states together.
  • Engine: estimateHpAndDps() takes an 8th argument, droneStates ({typeId: count in space}), and returns result.droneStates. That gives each drone type with its count in space, bandwidth used against the total, drones in space against the maximum, and refused requests. A request over a limit is granted in DPS order, and the rest is refused with the reason.

Verification

  • Expected values come from tools/independent_checks/check_drone_states.py. Per drone: Ogre II 63.36 DPS, Hammerhead II 31.68 DPS.
  • Four new constructed golden fits, all on an Abaddon with 3 Ogre II, 5 Hammerhead II and 2 Hornet EC-300:
    • default: 3 Ogre II, 190.1 DPS, 75/75 Mbit/s
    • 1 Ogre II + 3 Hammerhead II + 1 EC-300: 158.4 DPS, 60 Mbit/s, 5 in space
    • over bandwidth: the Hammerhead II is refused
    • over the 5-drone limit: the EC-300s are refused
    v.22 fails 2 checks on them; v.23 passes 143/143, and the parse tests pass 280/280.
  • Playwright click-through on the real Fit Analyzer page:
    • Locked squares don't respond.
    • With two Ogres taken back, exactly 4 Hammerhead squares unlock: there is bandwidth for 5, but only 4 drone slots are free.
    • DPS goes 190 → 127 → 63 → 158, and Reset brings back 190.
    • No console errors.

Fighters still use the automatic best case (launch tubes, per-class limits); they're in the backlog for the same treatment.
