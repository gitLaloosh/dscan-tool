What you get

  • Pilots: with "pilot rows up to" (15) or fewer hostile + neutral pilots in Local, the System Analyzer shows one row per pilot instead of the columns; a Pilots / Groups switch on the summary line brings the columns back.
  • Each row: the pilot (side, alliance ticker, corporation); the likely ship with its class and why ("on a kill 2 h ago", "lost one 3 d ago", "on scan; …", "2 of 3 losses, latest 40 d ago"); DPS with a bar by damage type; EHP; the weakest resist; the EWAR letters of the EWAR / Misc card; ✓ on scan. Click a row for the Pilot Analyzer on that pilot and hull. On a phone each row is a small card.
  • Likely ship: the newest ship they were seen in within 30 days (on one of their last 5 kills, or lost in one of their last 10 losses), a ship of theirs on your D-Scan first; nothing that recent: the hull they lost most. Capsules, shuttles, rookie ships and MTUs don't count.
  • The numbers come from their newest loss in that hull, worked out as in the Pilot Analyzer (pyfa's numbers). If they haven't lost one among those 10 losses, the row shows the bare hull's EHP and resists, marked fit unknown, with no DPS. A pilot with no zKillboard history says so.
  • zKillboard: 2 requests per pilot (kills, losses), kept an hour, through the page's queue (1 s apart); 15 pilots per paste, then Load N more pilots. Rows fill in place in the order asked (hostile first), then sort once: hostile first, then DPS.
  • No number the engine works out changed.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #74: a request log shared by several pages counts the other pages' work too. The check had just opened the Pilot Analyzer on its first page; that page's queued zKillboard request landed in the shared mock log after the check cleared it for the second page (31 instead of 30). Count only the requests that belong to what you're testing, or give each page its own context and log: clearing a list doesn't stop work already in flight.
