What you get

  • Against Your Ship, a new card: under "Their fleet, estimated" in the Fleet view, above the rows in the Pilots view. It uses the ship in My Ship; with none saved it asks for one, and with "Use in assessments" off it says so.
  • Settings on one line: the range (5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 50 km, starting at your guns' best range), you Still or with your MWD on, and All (≈ counts) or On Scan (only what's on your D-Scan).
  • A verdict first: "Don't take this fight." when you'd go before the first ship on the kill list breaks; otherwise how many you'd break first, or that you hold.
  • The numbers: how long you last (with the other count's time beside), the DPS that lands on you by damage type, how many ships hold you (T: scram or web in reach; C: neuts or nosferatus), how long your capacitor lasts under their neuts, their logistics.
  • Kill first: logistics, then the damage each puts on you per second of your fire; each row with its count, its DPS on you, the time you take to break one, and why.
  • Every ship's numbers are the Matchup's (pyfa's damage application both ways); your capacitor is pyfa's simulation with their neuts added the way pyfa adds projected neuts (their falloff counts). No number the engine works out changed.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #76: before modelling an effect, find pyfa's handler for it. The first draft counted a neut only inside its optimal, at full strength; pyfa's projected neut / nosferatu effects already feed the capacitor simulation through Fit.addDrain, with the falloff range factor (and the target's Energy Warfare Resistance and a capital neut's signature resolution, listed in DEVIATIONS.md as not applied yet). At 30 km the mock's Curse neut is in its falloff, so the made-up rule would have been wrong there; the check tests that range and a mutant with the optimal-only rule fails.
