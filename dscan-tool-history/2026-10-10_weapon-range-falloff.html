Weapon effective range / falloff

Date: 2026-10-10   Status: Delivered, verified via 28/28 regression PASS + a 6/6 direct unit test against hand-derived numbers (real ESI dogma) + a real-Chromium render check

Requested by the owner: the second half of "#1" — effective weapon range alongside the DPS-to-break-tank figure. Scoped over a feasibility pass (verified live ESI attributes for turret optimal/falloff and missile velocity/flight-time) and a scoping call where the owner chose to defer the drone-range question — this feature covers turrets and missiles only; drone engagement range is left to the pilot, as scoped.

The change

Each turret or launcher with a charge confirmed loaded at time of death (i.e. already feeding into the "DPS breakdown by source" list, not a cargo-ammo guess) now also gets an effective-range figure in a new "Effective range (confirmed loadout)" block:

  • Turrets: optimal range and falloff, each the weapon's own base value scaled by whatever ammo was actually loaded — optimal = maxRange × weaponRangeMultiplier, falloff = falloff × fallofMultiplier (both multipliers default to 1.0 when the ammo doesn't modify that stat). Verified live against Neutron Blaster Cannon II (7200m/10000m bare) with Void L loaded (0.75/0.5 multipliers, matching Void's own in-game tooltip).
  • Missiles: a single max-travel-distance figure, velocity × flight time, both attributes living on the missile/ammo item itself rather than the launcher. Verified live against Scourge Heavy Missile (4300 m/s, 6500ms flight time → ~28km), matching the known real heavy-missile range.

Deliberately not modeled: drone range (deferred by the owner), weapons only resolved via the cargo-ammo guess (no confirmed charge to compute from), and missile damage-application efficiency (explosion radius/velocity vs. a target's signature and speed) — that's a separate mechanic from travel distance and isn't conflated with it here. Zero new ESI calls — reuses the exact dogma already being fetched for DPS.
