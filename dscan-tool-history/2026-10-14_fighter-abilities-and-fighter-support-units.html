Fighters: every ability pyfa counts, plus Fighter Support Units

Bug fixPYFA CORE 2026-10-14 · v2026.10.14.3 → v2026.10.14.4 · Erik's pyfa check of the v.3 Thanatos fit, then "ok"

What Erik's screenshot showed

Leviathan (1,155) and Akoman (108) matched pyfa. The Thanatos with 3× Drone Damage Amplifier II and one Firbolg I squadron showed 831 DPS, volley 7,125 in pyfa; the tool gave 502 per squadron. Worked out from pyfa's source, the difference is exactly the second ability:

Attack Missile (pyfa "Turret Attack") — the only ability the tool counted2,508.7501.74

pyfa total7,124.7831.46

Root cause

  • The engine read one ability, the one ESI marks as default. pyfa (eos/saveddata/fighter.py Fighter.__init__) switches on Attack Missile and every implemented ability before it, except MWD and Evasive Maneuvers; getDpsPerEffect sums them. So:
    • light fighters fire Attack Missile and Missiles
    • superiority fighters (Equite, Locust, Satyr, Gram) fire Missiles only; the tool showed 0
    • long-range heavies (Cyclops, Malleus, Tyrfing, Mantis) fire both
    • heavy bombers (Ametat, Termite, Antaeus, Gungnir): Attack Missile only, because Launch Bomb comes after it and stays off
  • pyfa's DPS ignores rearm time unless "factor in reload time" is on, so the tool does too.
  • Fighter Support Units weren't applied at all. pyfa Effect6566 multiplies every ability's duration by 0.95 (T1) or 0.94 (T2), stacking-penalized in its own group.
  • Why it lasted: three fighter goldens from 2026-10-08 and 10-11 said "pyfa has no fighter-DPS support at all" and were pinned to hand derivations of the same one-ability model.

What changed

  • Generator (gen_hull_damage_table.py): hull rows for the Missiles ability (target fighterMissiles, 8 new rows on carriers, supercarriers and Caiman/Komodo). It also writes the new FIGHTER_ABILITY_EFFECTS table and asserts every damaging ability pyfa switches on by default is modeled. It checks that every fighter's effects are in effect-ID order, which is the order pyfa walks them.
  • PYFA CORE hull-damage: pyfaDefaultFighterAbilities() replays pyfa's activation rule; hullFighterMods takes the ability. Re-verified and re-recorded with --update.
  • Engine: per squadron, volley and DPS are the sums over the active abilities. Fighter Support Units are collected by effect 6566 and applied with pyfaPenalizedProduct.
  • Drone and fighter damage-module stacking now uses pyfa's exact penalty. With the rounded table the screenshot fit showed 832 (831.51 vs 831.46). No drone fit moved.
  • Correction to the v.3 doc: the Caiman / Komodo +100% bonus was never missing. Its "turret attack" attribute isn't read by any pyfa ability, and the same effect's Attack Missile part was already applied. It now applies to Missiles too.

Not changed

  • An EFT line Firbolg I x18 means 18 fighters in the bay to the tool, launched as three squadrons; pyfa loads one squadron of 6. This is an input choice, listed in DEVIATIONS.md.
  • Bombs: off by default in pyfa too.

Lesson for future models

When an item has several abilities or effects, read the pyfa code that decides which are on and the method that adds them up, not just the "default" one. A note saying "pyfa doesn't do X" is a claim about the source: open the source. See LESSONS_LEARNED.md #24 (and #5, #20).
