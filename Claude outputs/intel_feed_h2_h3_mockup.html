Intel Feed: H2 and H3 with your five changes

The real v2026.10.14.99 card with made-up pilots, groups and lines, and the same changes on both. The Y-OMTZ report is shown opened. Logos show as empty circles because the mock has no image server.

  1. Original line behind a toggle. A small chevron at the right of each report's title line. It opens an H4-style strip along the bottom: who sent it, the line as typed, and the channel. The copy-shape button moves into that strip. Kills don't get the toggle because they have no typed line.
  2. System name in its security colour. The colour comes from the system's rounded security, on EVE's 1.0 to 0.0 scale (below). Every system in this test data is in Delve, so they all show the nullsec colour. Jita is there to show a high-sec one. Near You uses the same colours.
  3. "x Jumps" in the quiet grey: "1 Jump", "3 Jumps", and "0 Jumps" for your own system. Near You now uses the same wording and grey (no red or amber by distance). On a phone the jumps go under the system name.
  4. Standing icons, EVE style. Each is a small coloured square with a white symbol and a slight shadow. Your corporation and your alliance get a star, which the old marks couldn't show. Neutral gets an "=" instead of the empty ring.
  5. Kills by who died. The label says Friendly lost, Hostile killed or Neutral killed, tinted with the victim's standing colour. H2 washes the whole panel in that colour. H3 uses it for the left stripe.
