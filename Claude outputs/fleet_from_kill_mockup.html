How it would work (proposed)

  • Who: every player attacker on the killmail with a ship; NPCs, capsules and pilots with no ship left out (said in the header: "12 pilots on the kill, 11 in ships").
  • Each fit: the Pilot Analyzer's rule for a chosen hull: their newest loss in that hull; none, their alliance's (else corporation's) common fit, said as not theirs; none, "fit unknown" (EHP of the bare hull, no DPS counted). One zKillboard request per pilot, through the page's queue, 15 at a time with Load more for a big fleet; the summary fills in as they come and says how many are looked up.
  • Sharing: the link carries the kill and, for each pilot, the loss their fit came from, so everyone who opens it sees the same fleet with no zKillboard requests for fits (the killmails come from ESI). The tab opens straight away from a shared link.
  • Order: the pilot whose dossier you came from first, then by DPS.
  • Not yet: the victim's side, and an "against your ship" verdict (later, once you've used it).
