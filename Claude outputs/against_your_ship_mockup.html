System Analyzer, step 4 (L5): their fleet against your ship

Drawn on the real v2026.10.14.84 page (the fleet view from its checks; the new card and all its numbers are mocked). With a ship saved in My Ship, the fleet view gets an Against your ship card under “Their fleet, estimated”. Against a fleet the answer is usually seconds, so the card leads with a plain verdict, then what kills you, what holds you, and what to shoot first.

How it’s worked out (proposed)

  • Their DPS on you: each counted hull’s common fit (the picked variant) applied to your ship at that range, as the Damage Application card does for one ship (pyfa’s damage application: your signature and speed, their webs and paints), × its ≈ count, added up by damage type.
  • You last: that damage through your resists, your reps and capacitor, by the same rule as the Matchup’s “you last” (the tool’s own fight math built from pyfa’s numbers, DEVIATIONS.md section 3).
  • Holding you: the counted ships whose scram, web or neut reaches you at that range. Your capacitor: pyfa’s capacitor simulation of your fit with their neuts’ drain added.
  • Kill first: for each hull, the damage it puts on you ÷ the time your fit takes to break one (your DPS applied to it, its EHP and resists, as the Matchup’s “they last”); highest first. Logistics would go above everything with “repairs the others”.
  • The verdict: “Don’t take this fight” when you’d be dead before you break the first ship on the kill list; otherwise how many you’d break first. A judgment from estimates, said as one.
