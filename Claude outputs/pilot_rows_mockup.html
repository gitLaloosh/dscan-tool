System Analyzer, step 2 (L3): one row per pilot in a small gang

Drawn on the real v2026.10.14.81 page (made-up pilots; the row contents are mocked). When local has 15 or fewer hostile + neutral pilots (your "pilot rows up to" number), each gets a row: what they likely fly and what that fit does. Rows fill in as zKillboard answers. Each row opens that pilot in the Pilot Analyzer.

1. Option A: the rows replace the columns (a Pilots / Groups switch)

2. Option B: rows above the columns

3. Phone, and "load more"

How each row is worked out (proposed)

  • zKillboard: 2 requests per pilot (their recent kills and losses), through the page's queue (1 s apart), kept an hour; the killmails come from ESI, kept forever. 15 pilots = 30 requests, about 30 s for every row; the hostile rows go first.
  • Likely ship (Q2): the ship they were seen in most recently, on a kill (the ship they flew) or a loss, within 30 days; a ship of theirs that is on your D-Scan wins. Older than 30 days: the hull they've lost most (the Pilot Analyzer's rule today).
  • The numbers (Q3): from their newest loss in that hull, through the same engine as the Pilot Analyzer (pyfa's numbers). If they have no loss in that hull, there's no fit to read, so the row shows the hull's own resists and EHP, with "fit unknown" and no DPS. The common fit for their group (D5) arrives with the fleet view.
