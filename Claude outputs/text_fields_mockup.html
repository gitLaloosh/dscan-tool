Text fields — today vs proposed

Real page styles, made-up text. Click into any field to see its focus; the second row shows every field "focused" at once.

Today, three different focus looks: most fields turn their border amber (the only amber focus on the page; buttons and switches use cyan); the Intel search shows the browser's own ring (its border rule overrides the page's); the Intel lists show nothing at all when focused (a keyboard user can't see where they are). Corners differ too: 3px in most places, 6px in the Intel filter bar; heights 36 or 28.
  2. Proposed, one text field: dark fill, a quiet border, 4px corners (the tag rule); hover a little brighter; focus = cyan border + a soft cyan ring (the one accent); disabled 45%. Placeholder text dimmer than typed text.
  3. Two sizes only: 36 px everywhere, 32 px in filter bars. Paste boxes keep the monospace font so pasted fits and D-Scan lines up.
  4. Not decided here: an error state (red border?) waits for the colour inventory's status colours.
