Pilot chip hover: B, with the corporation and alliance apart

Option B (outline and dividers on the whole chip, the part under the pointer filled), with the corporation and the alliance as targets of their own. Three things can be pressed: the name (opens the Pilot Analyzer), the corporation and the alliance. The standing icon only shows a tip; it can't be changed from the chip. Pressing a corporation or an alliance shows only that group in the Feed; press it again (or the × on the filter) to stop. Two ways to lay them out below.

Made-up pilots and groups; the logos are drawn stand-ins. Hover colours are white tints and each pilot's standing colour, never cyan (cyan means a link that leaves the site). A pilot with no alliance has no alliance target. Standings are set in the Standings panel today; once the EVE login is in, they come from your corporation and alliance.

B1B, today's order kept: a group's logo and ticker light up together

The chip keeps the order the page uses today (from the v.99 mockup): standing, corporation logo, alliance logo, name, [CORP] <ALLIANCE>. Point at the corporation's logo or its ticker and both fill; the same for the alliance. Either press shows only that group in the Feed.
