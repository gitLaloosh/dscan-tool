How it’s worked out (proposed)

  • Lately flying (Q1): the group’s 50 newest kills on zKillboard (1 request; the killmails from ESI, kept forever); every pilot of theirs on those kills counts the hull they flew. Kills show what they bring to a fight; losses lean to tackle and to the ones that died.
  • ≈ in local (Q2): the hull’s share × how many of the group are in local, rounded. An estimate, labeled so: the scan count beside it is what’s really near you.
  • Their fleet, estimated (Q3): the looked-up groups added up: raw DPS by damage type (no application, no resists), what most of their ships are weakest to, how many tacklers / EWAR / neuts and logistics that makes.
  • Against your ship (L5) is the next step: their DPS applied to your ship (My Ship), how long you last, what to kill first (Q4).
