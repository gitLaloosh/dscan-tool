Where the numbers come from

  • Affiliation: ESI /characters/{id}/ (corporation, alliance, birthday, security status) and /characters/{id}/corporationhistory/ (when they joined); names, tickers and logos as the System Analyzer already gets them (kept a week).
  • zKillboard: one request per pilot, /api/stats/characterID/{id}/, through the tool’s zKillboard queue (1 s apart, 100 per 10 minutes), kept an hour. zKillboard publishes its Danger and Gang ratios and average gang size for all time only. Exact field names get checked against the live API when it’s built; these are zKillboard’s numbers, shown as zKillboard shows them, not the tool’s.
