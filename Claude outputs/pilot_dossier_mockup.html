How it’s worked out (proposed)

  • Gang size on a kill = the player attackers on that killmail (NPCs left out; it can include third parties who joined in). Their losses show what they fly but not their own gang size (only who killed them), so losses count toward a hull, not toward a size.
  • Weapons seen: each killmail records the attacker’s ship and the weapon credited (a turret, launcher, drone or bomb). Flies with: the other attackers’ hulls most often on their kills in that hull. Kills mostly: the victims’ ship classes.
  • Active hours, usual systems: from their kills’ times and systems (losses too for systems).
  • Requests: no new zKillboard requests (their kills and losses lists are already asked); about 50 kills and 30 losses read from ESI, kept for good, so only a pilot’s first look is slower. A hull they’ve never lost: their alliance’s common fit (one zKillboard request, shared, kept an hour), or “fit unknown”.
  • Thin data stays visible: every number is a count, so a pilot with 6 kills shows 6, not a confident percentage.
  • zKillboard may label each kill with its own size group (solo, 5+, 10+ ...); if its lists carry those labels the tool could use its groups instead. I couldn’t confirm the field from here; counting attackers works either way.
