1. What I checked about the data (N2)

  • The window in your screenshot matches RIFT's Intel Feed (RIFT Intel Fusion Tool): its docs say the feed shows "live intel, whether from intel reports or killboard feeds", with coloring "based on your standings". RIFT is a desktop app; the kills come from zKillboard. I found nothing saying the EVE client itself has a kill feed a page could read.
  • zKillboard's live feed is R2Z2 (it replaced RedisQ, shut down 31 May 2026). zKillboard's API docs: read r2z2.zkillboard.com/ephemeral/sequence.json, then fetch each numbered killmail in turn until a 404; "Filter locally after retrieval"; "After a 404, wait at least six seconds"; "up to 15 requests per second per IP and requires a non-empty User-Agent" (a browser always sends one); files kept at least 24 hours.
  • It works from the tool's page. I tried it from the GitHub Pages address in your browser: R2Z2 answers the page directly (the browser allows it), and each record carries the killmail with its solar_system_id, the victim, every attacker, and zKillboard's values (ISK, npc, solo, attacker count).
  • The cost: zKillboard has 13,000-20,000 kills a day (its archive totals for 18-25 Oct), so following the feed is about 10-15 small requests a minute (about 2.5 KB each, roughly 3 MB an hour), one at a time, only while Intel is on. They wouldn't count against the limit the tool keeps for zKillboard's API (100 per 10 minutes): R2Z2 is a separate service with its own limit. The page keeps only kills within your range and drops NPC kills.
