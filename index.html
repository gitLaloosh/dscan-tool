Pilot Analyzer

Enter a pilot's name and, optionally, a ship type (from the scan above, or typed manually). Leave the ship blank and it'll sample their recent losses to guess which hull they fly most.

Fit Analyzer

Paste a fit copied from the EVE client's fitting window (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C in the Fitting window) or exported from pyfa as EFT format. Shows the same cards as the Pilot Analyzer, just for any fit you give it directly instead of a specific killmail.
